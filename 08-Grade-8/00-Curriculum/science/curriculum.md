# Science - Grade 8

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 8](../README.md) > Science

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial expectations. See the [program context](../README.md#choose-a-program).

**Document language:** English (en-CA). The summary language does not determine classroom language.

**Learning objective:** Investigate living systems, matter, light, and Earth processes, connecting models and evidence with local environments and the consequences of scientific knowledge.

**Prerequisites:** Previous experience observing, measuring, representing data, and working with scientific models is helpful, not a formal course-entry condition imposed here.

**Materials:** No laboratory supplies are required to read this reference. Practical investigations need separately planned safety, ethical, equipment, and accessibility arrangements.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for the official standards and elaborations. The local item IDs support traceable mappings and are not ministry standard codes or laboratory instructions.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Cells provide the scale at which the processes that sustain life can be investigated.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, cellular life processes.

### BI-02

Particle-motion and atomic models help explain observable behaviour of matter, while both wave and particle descriptions contribute to understanding how energy is transferred.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, kinetic molecular/atomic theory and particle/wave energy transfer.

### BI-03

Plate tectonics connects many apparently separate geological processes in a common explanation of Earth's changing surface.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, plate tectonics as a unifying geological theory.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Develop sustained curiosity into investigable questions. Use observations to identify a problem, propose alternative conditional hypotheses, and predict findings.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Questioning and predicting.

Plan investigations collaboratively, including experiments and field work. Control and measure variables in fair tests; gather qualitative and quantitative evidence accurately and precisely, use SI units and simple conversions, and follow safety and ethical guidelines.

## Content

### C-01

Study the characteristics of living things through cell theory and different cell types. Relate cell structures to life functions, including photosynthesis and cellular respiration; distinguish plant and animal cells and prokaryotic and eukaryotic organization.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, characteristics of life; cell theory and types of cells, including their elaborations.

### C-02

Investigate relationships between micro-organisms and other living things, including beneficial functions and disease. Connect basic immune responses, vaccination, and antibiotics with the effects of epidemics and pandemics on populations.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, micro-organisms; immune system; vaccination and antibiotics; epidemics and pandemics.

### C-03

Use kinetic molecular and atomic theories to model matter. Relate particle arrangement and motion to physical states, and distinguish atomic constituents, including protons and neutrons composed of quarks and electrons as leptons.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, kinetic molecular theory; atomic theory and models; protons/neutrons/quarks; electrons/leptons.

### C-04

Compare types of electromagnetic radiation and their effects. Investigate light's properties and behaviours, including wave characteristics, interactions with materials, image formation, and biological or technological ways of sensing light.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, electromagnetic radiation; light properties, behaviours, and ways of sensing, with elaborations.

### C-05

Connect Earth's layers, plate motion, and plate boundaries with earthquakes, volcanoes, and significant local geological events. Include First Peoples knowledge of local formations and geological events as part of this study.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, plate tectonic movement; major local geological events; First Peoples geological knowledge; layers of Earth.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The source distinguishes the use of models from direct observation and accuracy from precision. Its atomic content includes quarks and leptons; this is not a requirement for advanced particle-physics calculations. The light elaborations encompass reflection, refraction, absorption, transmission, scattering, lenses, and mirrors rather than only naming colours or the spectrum.

For health-related content, the source distinguishes antibiotics acting against bacteria from treatment of viral infections and notes antibiotic resistance. These are scientific learning topics, not personal medical advice. Its discussion of population impacts includes the smallpox epidemic's effects on First Peoples; do not detach disease history from its human context.

First Peoples geological knowledge and perspectives of place are within the curriculum, not optional enrichment added outside science. Access to community knowledge requires suitable relationships, attribution, and permissions. Suggested inquiry questions and examples remain at [S1] and are not all prescribed investigations.
