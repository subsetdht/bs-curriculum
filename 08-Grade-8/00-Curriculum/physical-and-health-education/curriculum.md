# Physical and Health Education - Grade 8

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 8](../README.md) > Physical and Health Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial expectations. See the [program context](../README.md#choose-a-program).

**Document language:** English (en-CA), separate from the language of instruction.

**Learning objective:** Develop movement competence and sustainable health practices while learning to support consent, safety, relationships, and personal and community well-being.

**Prerequisites:** Earlier movement and health-learning experiences are helpful; athletic selection or a specified fitness level is not a prerequisite established here.

**Materials:** No sports equipment, health disclosure, or personal fitness data is needed to read this reference. Activities require their own accessible, appropriately supervised arrangements.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for official standards and elaborations. Local IDs are repository references, not official codes, a training plan, a diagnosis, or an assessment of an individual learner.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Regular participation in varied movement develops physical literacy and supports personal health and fitness. Remaining active over a lifetime brings continuing benefits.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, daily varied activity and lifelong physical participation.

### BI-02

Health choices affect physical, emotional, and mental well-being, while supportive relationships contribute to a fulfilling life.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, healthy choices and healthy relationships.

### BI-03

Supporting other people's health and well-being is also a way to participate in and strengthen a community.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, advocacy and community connection.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Refine and apply movement skills, concepts, and strategies in different activities and environments. Monitor and adjust exertion, practise safety and fair play, demonstrate leadership, and identify preferred forms of activity.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Physical literacy.

### CC-02

Participate daily in activity that supports health-related fitness. Explain how movement at school, at home, and in the community influences health and fitness.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Healthy and active living, daily participation and activity contexts.

### CC-03

Develop strategies for healthy eating and personal healthy-living goals. Assess influences on health choices and messages, then reflect on outcomes and the effectiveness of the strategies used.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Healthy and active living, eating, health messages/choices, and goal-setting/reflection.

### CC-04

Propose ways to avoid or respond to unsafe, abusive, or exploitative situations and to discrimination, stereotyping, and bullying. Use understanding of boundaries, consent, power imbalances, and help-seeking to support safety without blaming people who experience harm.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Social and community health, unsafe situations and discrimination/bullying, including elaborations.

### CC-05

Develop strategies for respectful, healthy relationships and for improving the health and well-being of the school and wider community.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Social and community health, relationships and school/community well-being.

### CC-06

Assess ways to support mental well-being for oneself and others, including ways of helping others with problems involving mental health or substance use. Consider available support rather than taking on a diagnostic or treatment role.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Mental well-being, promotion and management of mental-well-being/substance-use problems; support-resource elaboration.

### CC-07

Create and assess strategies for responding to physical, emotional, and social changes in adolescence. Explore how transition and change can influence identity and relationships.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Mental well-being, puberty/adolescence and identity transitions.

## Content

### C-01

Develop sound technique in non-travelling, travelling, and object-control movement skills. Relate body and spatial awareness, effort, and relationships to strategies for individual or paired activities, rhythmic activities, and games.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, fundamental movement skills; movement concepts/strategies; types of physical activity.

### C-02

Understand how to monitor exertion and how different activities affect the body. Connect fitness planning with frequency, intensity, type, and time (FITT), specific adaptation to imposed demand (SAID), and training specificity.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, exertion levels; training principles; effects of activity on the body.

### C-03

Examine short- and long-term consequences of health decisions involving nutrition, sleep, sexual decision making, and protection from sexually transmitted infections. Consider consent and boundaries, sources of health information, and the influence of marketing to young people, including food and supplement advertising.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, healthy sexual decision making; marketing/advertising; consequences of health decisions; health-information sources.

### C-04

Understand basic emergency responses and ways to protect oneself and others from abuse, exploitation, and harm in different settings. This includes recognizing rights and boundaries, seeking help, and considering consent and risks in both digital and face-to-face situations.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, responding to emergencies; protection from abuse, exploitation, and harm, with elaborations.

### C-05

Consider the consequences of bullying, stereotyping, and discrimination, alongside the ways media and social influences can shape psychoactive-substance use and potentially addictive behaviour.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, bullying/stereotyping/discrimination; media/social influences on substance use and addictive behaviours.

### C-06

Recognize signs associated with stress, anxiety, and depression. Understand how physical, emotional, and social changes can affect self-understanding, boundaries, and relationships.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, signs and symptoms of stress/anxiety/depression; changes affecting identities and relationships.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Health education and physical education are both integral to this record. The source's **daily participation** expectation should not be converted into an invented number of minutes, a particular sport requirement, or a uniform performance threshold. Examples such as traditional First Peoples games remain examples, with appropriate community context and permissions.

Consent, sexual health, substance-use influences, and mental well-being are age-appropriate curriculum topics, not optional omissions from a movement-only summary. The source's elaborations emphasize ongoing, affirmative consent, respect for boundaries, help-seeking, and the fact that survivors are not to blame. This reference contains no explicit scenarios, personal disclosures, or medical instructions.

Use official elaborations for detail and current school arrangements for delivery and supports. Neither an example activity nor a list of possible symptoms is a compulsory exercise or an individual diagnosis.
