# Career Education - Grade 8

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 8](../README.md) > Career Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial expectations. See the [program context](../README.md#choose-a-program).

**Document language:** English (en-CA), separate from classroom language.

**Learning objective:** Explore possible life and work pathways through self-knowledge, community connections, research, and adaptable planning.

**Prerequisites:** Previous reflection, goal-setting, and collaborative experiences are useful, not formal entry requirements.

**Materials:** No special materials or personal assessment data are needed to use this reference.

**How to use:** Read [S1] alongside the **Grades 8-9** section of [S2]. Grade 8 is the repository placement, not a newly invented set of Grade-8-only standards. Local IDs are repository references, not official codes.

These standards belong to the broader Grades 6-9 exploration phase described in [S3]. They are not the senior Career-Life Education or Career-Life Connections courses, and do not impose a capstone or the senior career-life exploration hours on Grade 8.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Knowing one's interests and capabilities helps turn future possibilities into manageable learning goals. Progress involves both effort and the willingness to continue when learning is difficult.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, reflection on preferences/skills and perseverance toward learning goals; [S2], Grades 8-9.

### BI-02

Work can have different meanings for individuals and communities. Career directions emerge through personal choices, educational experiences, and relationships with others.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, diverse perspectives on work and personal/community/educational choices.

### BI-03

Changing economic and employment conditions call for flexible plans rather than a permanently fixed career decision.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, adaptation to economic and labour-market change.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Use self-assessment and reflection to identify strengths and preferences, set realistic learning goals, and work toward them with resilience. Recognize how one's public presentation, including a digital presence, can affect work opportunities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, self-assessment, public identity, and realistic learning goals.

### CC-02

Explore how personal interests can contribute to local and global needs. Question assumptions about careers and examine how family traditions, community expectations, and different views of work influence choices.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, purposes/passions and community needs; diverse perspectives on work; family expectations and traditions.

### CC-03

Solve problems with respect, inclusion, and collaboration. Demonstrate safety skills and recognize why workplace safety matters.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, inclusive collaboration and workplace safety.

### CC-04

Research varied career possibilities and related career clusters, using a range of information-gathering approaches. Consider how school subjects, activities, mentors, and personal networks open different pathways.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, curriculum/co-curricular choices; mentors and resources; research and career clusters.

### CC-05

Explore volunteering and unfamiliar learning experiences for opportunities to create or improve ideas. Apply decision-making strategies to life, work, or community problems and adapt those strategies as circumstances change.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, volunteering/entrepreneurial and innovative thinking; adaptable decision making.

## Content

### C-01

Bring together self-assessment, reflection, and goal-setting to guide career research and personal development. Understand project management as planning resources and time, acting on a plan, and reviewing the process.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Personal Development; [S2], Grades 8-9 project-management elaboration.

### C-02

Connect community needs and opportunities with cultural and social awareness, the factors shaping local employment, and the value of volunteering.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community.

### C-03

Build awareness of graduation requirements and the people and networks that support planning and decisions. Consider how technology changes learning and working.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Life and Career Plan, graduation requirements, networks/mentorship, and technology.

### C-04

Understand workplace safety through hazard assessment and control, workers' rights and responsibilities, and emergency procedures.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Life and Career Plan, workplace safety and its three subtopics.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The official compilation labels these expectations **Grades 8-9**. Do not infer an official split of topics between the two years or treat the broader Grades 6-9 exploration phase as the standards' grade band.

Learning about graduation requirements is part of planning; it does not make graduation requirements due in Grade 8. The source does not set a mandatory number of volunteer hours or require a single career choice. Its examples include multiple pathways beyond school rather than privileging one route.

The career introduction [S3] places experiential, reflective learning and community relationships in the context of First Peoples Principles of Learning. Elders, stories, and community knowledge can inform career exploration with appropriate local relationships and permissions; no cultural work or learner's personal profile is reproduced here.
