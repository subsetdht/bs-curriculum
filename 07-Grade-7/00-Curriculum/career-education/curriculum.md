# Career Education - Grade 7

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 7](../README.md) > [Career Education](README.md)

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial expectations. The parallel-content requirement for immersion is described in [S3]. The Required Areas Order [S4], sections 1-2, includes Francophone students under the same Career Education guide, subject to the individual exceptions in section 3.

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`); the reviewed source is English. Document language does not establish instructional language.

**Learning objective:** Connect self-knowledge, relationships, community contribution, practical planning, and new experiences with developing career possibilities.

**Prerequisites:** Everyday learning and community experiences provide starting points. Prior employment, a chosen occupation, or a particular family career history is not required.

**Materials:** None are needed for this reference. Any future activity should identify its own materials and avoid requiring private employment, financial, or personal information.

**How to use:** This record applies the official **Grades 6-7** band to Grade 7; the compilation [S2] establishes the band. Read the main-page standards and elaborations [S1] rather than importing the following Grades 8-9 band. Local IDs are repository references, not official standards or a career-placement instrument.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Career possibilities are interpreted through both self-understanding and relationships with other people. How one presents oneself online is part of a wider public identity.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, self/friends/family/community influences and digital/public identity.

### BI-02

Respectful, inclusive, and ethical participation helps prepare learners for shared work. Safety depends on everyone's conduct, while leadership brings people together through planning and goals.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, workplace behaviour, leadership, and shared responsibility for safety.

### BI-03

Experiences inside and outside school can uncover interests, build skills, and open paths that a learner has not yet considered.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, new experiences and expanding career skills/options.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Connect preferences, strengths, and abilities with possible directions in life and work. Examine how peers, family, and community influence work attitudes and choices, and consider both beneficial and harmful consequences of a public identity.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, self-knowledge, personal public identity, and influences on career choices/attitudes.

### CC-02

Investigate the responsibilities and benefits involved in contributing through service. Recognize the two-way relationship between individuals and communities and the importance of respectful, inclusive participation in diverse learning and work settings.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, service learning, reciprocal community relationships, and positive collaborative behaviours.

### CC-03

Use creative, innovative, and entrepreneurial thinking to solve problems and create possibilities. Value unfamiliar experiences and constructive exploration as ways of discovering additional interests and career options.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies and elaborations, entrepreneurial/innovative thinking and new experiences/risk-taking.

### CC-04

Demonstrate leadership in collaborative school and community work, and apply safety skills during experiential learning.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, collaborative leadership and safety.

### CC-05

Establish realistic near-term and longer-term learning goals, identify a route toward them, and monitor progress. Apply project-management skills to turn intentions into organized action and reflection.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, learning goals and progress; project management.

### CC-06

Explore volunteering and other experiences beyond school and identify what they can contribute to career development.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, volunteer opportunities and out-of-school experiences.

## Content

### C-01

**Personal Development:** Learn methods for self-assessment, setting goals, decision-making, and problem solving. Project management connects an idea with a plan for time and resources, action, and review; leadership helps coordinate that work with others.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content and elaborations, Personal Development.

### C-02

**Connections to Community:** Understand local and global needs, opportunities to contribute, and the responsibilities of citizenship. Cultural and social awareness includes recognizing differences and respecting Indigenous traditions; volunteering is one route for connecting these understandings with community life.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content and elaborations, Connections to Community: needs/opportunities, cultural/social awareness, global citizenship, and volunteering.

### C-03

**Life and Career Plan:** Examine what shapes the kinds of work available in a community, how technology affects learning and work, and how mentors and personal networks influence decisions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Life and Career Plan.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Career development here is exploration, not an obligation to choose an occupation or establish a business. The source's "risk-taking" means constructive exploration of new activities and interests, not unsafe work, financial risk, or disregard for safety procedures. No compulsory volunteer-hour total is specified by this source.

The elaborations describe learning as experiential, reflective, relational, and connected to place, and identify intergenerational roles and family/community well-being. Preserve those perspectives rather than reducing career learning to individual employment outcomes. Indigenous traditions are not interchangeable or unrestricted material for reuse.

Digital identity is broader than operating a social-media account; public communication and representation can be examined without requiring students to publish personal details or create accounts.

This grade-banded reference is not the senior Career-Life Education or Career-Life Connections curriculum and does not assign those named courses to Grade 7.
