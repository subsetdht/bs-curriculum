# CSF / Francophone context - Grade 7

[Curriculum index](../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 7](README.md) > CSF context

**Context:** Conseil scolaire francophone de la Colombie-Britannique (CSF), the Francophone education provider. **Document language:** English. **Source review date:** 2026-09-14.

This page separates provider delivery from provincial learning standards. CSF's first-language Francophone programme is distinct from Early and Late French Immersion; this is not SD43 guidance.

## Provincial standards and provider delivery

The [Required Areas of Learning Order][S1], sections 1-2, includes Francophone authorities, programmes, and students and establishes the same seven non-language learning areas. It identifies French Language Arts for Francophone students. Section 4(b) requires a language other than French in Grades 5-8, subject to the applicable individual and local-language provisions in sections 3 and 6. That clause does not itself prescribe English Language Arts 7 by name.

CSF's [elementary programme page][S2] states that it follows the provincial curriculum and provides **first-language English classes to all CSF students from Grade 4**. This provider policy supports including the provincial [English Language Arts 7 reference](english-language-arts/curriculum.md) in the reviewed CSF Grade 7 pathway alongside [Francophone Language Arts 7](francophone-language-arts/curriculum.md) and the shared subjects.

The standards in those records remain provincial. The decision described here about when CSF introduces English is provider delivery, not a newly inferred province-wide timetable or a rule for immersion programmes.

## Limits

The French source page and these English repository summaries do not make all CSF instruction English. No percentage allocation, term sequence, textbook choice, individual placement, or local service availability is established by this page.

The source also describes interventions for younger grades; they are not Grade 7 requirements and are not imported here. Graduation language requirements are likewise not used as evidence of Grade 7 scheduling.

Consult the [grade programme chooser](README.md#choose-a-program) for the separate provincial immersion provisions. Actual timetables and learner supports remain matters for the provider and school.
