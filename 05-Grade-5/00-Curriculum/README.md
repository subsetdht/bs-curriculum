**Coverage:** **19 source-checked records**: ten subject/program records and all nine published Grade 5 language-course choices. All existing Grade 5 catalog landings are populated. **Document language:** English (`en-CA`). **Alignment review date:** **2026-09-14**.

These are independent, condensed curriculum references, not official wording, lesson plans, a timetable, or an assessment checklist. A course's presence does not mean every learner must take it. Follow the program pathways and source locators before mapping learning material.

## Choose a program

[English-language program](#english-language-program) | [Early French Immersion](#early-french-immersion) | [Francophone](#francophone)

### English-language program

Start with [English Language Arts](english-language-arts/README.md) and the seven shared non-language subjects in the [subject table](#subjects-and-learning-standards). The ordinary Grade 5 pathway also includes a locally offered second language: the [Languages inventory](languages/README.md) has all **nine published choices**, not just Core French.

This pathway has eight subject records plus nine course-choice records available for reference; it does not require seventeen courses. The provincial order governs individual exceptions, and the board determines its language offerings.

### Early French Immersion

Use [French Immersion Language Arts](french-immersion-language-arts/README.md), [English Language Arts](english-language-arts/README.md), and the seven shared non-language subjects. **Both language-arts records belong here:** provincial policy provides ELA from Grade 4 onward. This is a nine-subject-record pathway, not the Grade 1 language schedule copied forward.

The eight non-French courses in the [language table](#language-course-choices) are additional options where selected; none is automatically required on top of the immersion program. Core French is not a substitute for immersion language arts.

### Francophone

Use [Francophone Language Arts](francophone-language-arts/README.md), the separate [English Language Arts](english-language-arts/README.md) component, and the seven shared non-language subjects. This pathway contains nine subject records and preserves the distinct first-language Francophone curriculum.

The ministry describes instruction in French apart from ELA. A language other than French is addressed in the provincial order; the eight non-French courses in the inventory are choices, not eight extra compulsory subjects. Core French does not replace French first-language arts or meet a non-French-language requirement.

**Late French Immersion:** Not a Grade 5 entry under the provincial model; the normal entry is Grade 6. No Grade 5 Late Immersion record or transition pathway is asserted.

All summaries are English reference documents. Their program applicability is not a claim that they are French-language instructional resources. See the [program basis](#program-basis) for provincial authority and the [implementation boundary](#local-implementation).

## Program basis

Program applicability was reviewed using provincial sources, not inferred from the language of this summary or from a French download button.

| Provincial source | Relevant scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Required Areas of Learning in an Educational Program Order][areas] | Section 1 includes Francophone authorities, programs, and students. Section 2 identifies the seven shared non-language areas while distinguishing language arts. Section 4 addresses English-program and Francophone second languages and ELA in immersion Grades 4-9. Sections 3, 5, and 6 provide specific exceptions. |
| [Educational Program Guide Order][guides] | Current order M231/19, section 2(a)-(b), specifies K-9 curriculum guides, including French documents for Francophone and immersion programs. The legacy `m333_99.pdf` filename is not the current order number. |
| [French Immersion Program policy][immersion-policy] | Describes curriculum parallel to the English program, Early Immersion entry in Kindergarten/1, Late Immersion entry at 6, and ELA from 4 onward in Early Immersion. Recommended time allocations are not a timetable imposed by this repository. |
| [Provincial French-program comparison][program-comparison] | Distinguishes Core French, immersion, and the Francophone program; describes French and English language arts in Francophone education and instruction in French apart from ELA. Broad eligibility and grade ranges do not make every French course compulsory. |
| [Language Education Policy][language-policy] | Explains board choice of second-language offerings and Core French as the default when there is no alternative. Read it with the current order, including the section 6 local Indigenous-language provision. |

All sources in this table were reviewed **2026-09-14**. The shared records cover ADST, Arts Education, Career Education, Mathematics, Physical and Health Education, Science, and Social Studies. ELA is shared as an English-literacy component; the two French language-arts curricula remain separate.

Exemptions under section 3 depend on the circumstances described in the order, not simply a disability, an IEP's existence, or ELL status. This index does not make individual decisions about placement, language requirements, or exemptions.

## How to use these references

Read a record's Scope and use section, then follow its locators to the authoritative Big Ideas, Curricular Competencies, and Content. The summaries group related expectations and preserve important boundaries rather than duplicate every official line or elaboration.

Item IDs are local and stable. For example, [Mathematics 5: C-08](mathematics/curriculum.md#c-08) has the canonical reference `bc-g05-mathematics#c-08`. Use a descriptive record/item link outside its record; a bare `C-08` is ambiguous and is not an official BC code.

ADST and Career Education use **Grades 4-5** standards. ADST combines those competencies with Grade 5 content from another subject rather than prescribing a separate technology syllabus. The other records use Grade 5 standards even when downloads collect several grades.

First Peoples perspectives remain within their relevant subjects and language courses. Curriculum inclusion does not grant permission to copy stories, designs, performances, or community-specific knowledge.

## Local implementation

School and district decisions about offerings, admission, scheduling, instruction, and materials are separate from these provincial references. No SD43-specific practice is presented as a BC-wide requirement. Families should use their own school or district's current guidance for implementation.

## Coverage and limits

The ten subject/program records and nine course records are all **core summaries** with `status: source-checked`. All eleven original Grade 5 catalog landings are filled; the Languages parent links to nine named course landings. There are no unresolved source-access or missing-summary blockers in this published Grade 5 inventory.

There are **17 English-language primary sources and two French-language primary sources**; all 19 repository documents are written in English. French-language editions remain unwritten. Source revision dates were not established and are recorded as `null`; the review date is not a new curriculum edition.

The nine-language inventory does not claim every locally developed or locally owned language curriculum or guarantee local offerings. See its [coverage limits](languages/README.md#coverage-and-limits). Late Immersion is not a missing Grade 5 program.

No lessons, assessments, resources, or tools were created. The [resources](../01-Resources), [tools](../02-Tools), and [additional-content](../03-Additional-Content) folders remain available for later work.
