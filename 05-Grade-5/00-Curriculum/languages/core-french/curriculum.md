# Core French - Grade 5

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [5](../../README.md) > [Languages](../README.md) > Core French

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language second-language pathway. This mapping is not a statement of individual admission eligibility; it distinguishes beginner Core French from the [immersion](../../french-immersion-language-arts/curriculum.md) and [Francophone](../../francophone-language-arts/curriculum.md) language-arts curricula.

**Document language:** English; the official source's directions are English with French examples. Learning concerns communication in French, not the language in which this reference is written.

**Learning objective:** Understand and exchange simple French messages about familiar people and interests while becoming aware of Canada's Francophone communities and cultures.

**Prerequisites:** No formal prior French proficiency is specified by this reference. Earlier exposure can help, and the source explicitly allows support in simple interactions.

**Materials:** None are needed to read the reference. French-language texts and communication supports should be selected separately for accessibility and appropriate use.

**How to use:** Read [S1] with the [course-selection notes](../README.md#course-selection-and-programs). The Grade 5 course is one choice, not all of Languages and not the entire 5-10 compilation. Local IDs are repository references, not ministry codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Attentive listening and viewing, together with spoken and non-verbal clues, can make unfamiliar French understandable.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, intentional listening/viewing and verbal/non-verbal cues.

### BI-02

Familiar words and sentence patterns can support an exchange in which learners describe themselves and their interests.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, simple self-description and reciprocal communication.

### BI-03

Stories support language development, while traditions and celebrations provide windows into cultural life.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, stories and cultural traditions.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Understand important information in slowly and clearly delivered speech, simple texts, and simple stories. Use non-verbal clues and recognize how intonation can change a message.

**Source locator:** [S1], Thinking and communicating, comprehension, stories, non-verbal cues, and intonation.

### CC-02

Use communication strategies, seek clarification, and respond to simple commands or instructions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Thinking and communicating, strategies, clarification, and instructions.

### CC-03

With support, participate in everyday exchanges and express and understand messages through formats suited to different abilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Thinking and communicating, supported interactions and modes of presentation.

### CC-04

Locate Francophone communities across Canada, recognize connections between First Peoples communities and French, and identify a Canadian Francophone celebration or festival.

**Source locator:** [S1], Personal and social awareness.

## Content

### C-01

Learn the French alphabet and speech sounds, alongside introductory distinctions of grammatical gender and singular/plural number.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, alphabet, phonemes, gender, and number.

### C-02

Build frequently used language for greetings, introductions, questions, personal information, descriptions, and expressions of likes, dislikes, preferences, and interests.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, high-frequency vocabulary and sentence structures for communicating meaning.

### C-03

Learn about French-speaking communities across Canada, including the First Peoples connections developed in the competency elaboration.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, communities where French is spoken; Personal and social awareness, First Peoples/French-language connections.

### C-04

Explore common features of cultural celebrations through a selected **Francophone festival or celebration in Canada**.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, common elements of celebrations and a Canadian Francophone festival/celebration.

### C-05

Consider the ethics of cultural appropriation and plagiarism when working with language, stories, images, and other cultural expression.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, cultural appropriation and plagiarism.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The source's model phrases, communities, and festivals are examples, not a required phrasebook or an obligation to attend every named event. The expectation to identify a Canadian Francophone celebration should not disappear into a generic statement about world cultures.

Core French is not a substitute for Grade 5 French language arts. Early Immersion also includes ELA, and French cannot itself satisfy the Francophone requirement for a language other than French. See the [program basis](../../README.md#program-basis) rather than treating every listed course as compulsory.
