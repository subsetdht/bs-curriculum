# Punjabi - Grade 5

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [5](../../README.md) > [Languages](../README.md) > Punjabi

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language; also a selected language-course option in Early French Immersion and Francophone education. The [program basis](../../README.md#program-basis) separates this authorization from local availability.

**Document language:** English; the source uses English explanations and Punjabi examples. This is not a Punjabi-language teaching edition.

**Learning objective:** Begin understanding and exchanging Punjabi messages while connecting sounds and Gurmukhi writing with meaning, culture, and community.

**Prerequisites:** No formal prior Punjabi proficiency or heritage-language background is required by this reference.

**Materials:** None are needed for reading. Later learning materials should provide suitable visual/audio access and observe rights and cultural permissions.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for Grade 5 and the [Languages inventory](../README.md) for course choice. The longer 5-10 compilation does not expand this record's grade scope. Local IDs are repository references, not ministry standard codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Listening and viewing with care helps learners use both language and non-verbal cues. Familiar patterns make a basic two-way exchange possible.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, listening/viewing, cues, and reciprocal communication.

### BI-02

A new language can support exploration of identity and understanding of cultural traditions and celebrations.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, identity and culture.

### BI-03

Stories offer a meaningful setting for acquiring language.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, stories.

## Content

### C-01

Explore Punjabi's origins and relationships between its symbols and speech sounds. The source introduces the **Gurmukhi script and alphabet**, including relevant phonemes and sound distinctions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, symbols/sounds, Punjabi alphabet, phonemes, and language origin.

### C-02

Use common language for questions, descriptions, and information about oneself and others. Learn Gurmukhi numerals and basic grammatical gender and number, and distinguish formal from informal address.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, high-frequency language, numerals, gender/number, and modes of address.

### C-03

Learn about Punjabi communities across Canada, artistic works, and common features of cultural festivals and celebrations.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Punjabi communities, works of art, and festivals/celebrations.

### C-04

Consider First Peoples understandings of connections among language, oral histories, identity, and place.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, First Peoples language/culture connections.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The source's script focus is Gurmukhi. It does not set a complete vocabulary inventory or a numerical mastery range for this course. Basic gender and number concern linguistic forms, not a requirement to classify learners' identities.

Communities, celebrations, and model phrases in the elaborations are examples, not a claim that every Punjabi speaker shares one religious or cultural practice. Respect permissions and avoid plagiarism or appropriation when using art and oral histories.
