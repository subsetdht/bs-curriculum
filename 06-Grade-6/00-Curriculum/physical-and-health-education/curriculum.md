# Physical and Health Education - Grade 6

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 6 overview](../README.md) > Physical and Health Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; shared non-language expectations under [S2], sections 1-2. See the [program overview](../README.md#choose-a-program) for instruction-language distinctions and applicable exceptions.

**Document language:** English; this does not prescribe classroom language.

**Learning objective:** Develop confident, safe movement and health decision-making while understanding relationships, personal change, and responsibility for individual and community well-being.

**Prerequisites:** Prior opportunities for varied movement and discussions about health are helpful. This reference requires no competitive-sport experience or fitness threshold.

**Materials:** No equipment is needed to read the reference. Physical activities require accessible arrangements, suitable supervision, and their own safety planning.

**How to use:** Read [S1] for official expectations and elaborations across physical literacy, healthy living, social/community health, and mental well-being. This is curriculum information, not personal medical advice, a training plan, or a request for private disclosures. Local IDs are repository references, not ministry standard codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Regular physical activity offers opportunities to refine movement and develop fitness; growing physical literacy and fitness can in turn support enjoyment and success in being active.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, daily physical activity and physical literacy/fitness.

### BI-02

Changes in life affect how people understand themselves and others. Health-related choices influence physical, emotional, and mental well-being.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, life changes and healthy choices.

### BI-03

Understanding similarities and differences among people helps shape the health of a community.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, individual/group similarities and differences and community health.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Refine and apply fundamental movement skills and strategies in different activities and environments. Monitor and adjust exertion, demonstrate safety, fairness, and leadership, and describe the kinds of activity one prefers.

**Source locator:** [S1], Physical literacy, movement skills/concepts, exertion, safe and fair participation, and preferences.

### CC-02

Participate daily in activity that supports health-related fitness and explain how participation across school, home, and community affects health. Pursue personal healthy-living goals and reflect on the strategies used.

**Source locator:** [S1], Healthy and active living, daily participation, health/fitness effects, and goal strategies.

### CC-03

Plan food choices with personal well-being in mind, describe the effects of choices, and analyze health messages and the intentions behind them.

**Source locator:** [S1], Healthy and active living, food choices, personal choices, and health messages.

### CC-04

Identify ways to avoid or respond to unsafe, abusive, or exploitative situations, and assess responses to bullying, stereotyping, and discrimination. Develop healthy relationships through respectful communication, boundaries, and consent, and explore ways to support school and community health.

**Source locator:** [S1], Social and community health, safety, discrimination, relationships, and community well-being, including consent elaborations.

### CC-05

Assess strategies that promote one's own and others' mental well-being and ways to respond to others' difficulties involving mental well-being or substance use, including appropriate sources of support.

**Source locator:** [S1], Mental well-being, promotion of well-being and managing problems related to mental well-being/substance use.

### CC-06

Explore ways to manage physical, emotional, and social changes during puberty and adolescence. Consider how personal identity can change across settings and relationships.

**Source locator:** [S1], Mental well-being, puberty/adolescence and identities in different settings.

## Content

### C-01

Develop proper technique for stationary movement, travelling movement, and object-control skills, using concepts of body, space, effort, and relationships. Learn ways to participate in individual or paired activities, rhythmic activity, and games.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, fundamental movement skills, movement concepts/strategies, and types of physical activity.

### C-02

Understand ways to gauge and adjust exertion and principles for improving fitness. FITT connects frequency, intensity, type, and time; SAID connects the body's adaptation with the demands placed on it.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, monitoring/adjusting exertion and training principles, including FITT and SAID.

### C-03

Examine influences on food choices and sources of health information, including how access, preferences, family, culture, and messages can affect decisions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, influences on food choices and sources of health information.

### C-04

Learn about practices that reduce the risk of sexually transmitted infections and life-threatening communicable diseases. Understand basic emergency-response principles, including safety, a response plan, and getting help.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, infection/disease risk reduction and basic principles for responding to emergencies.

### C-05

Understand protective strategies against abuse, exploitation, and harm, including consent, personal boundaries, and online safety. Examine the consequences of bullying, stereotyping, and discrimination and ways to manage risks involving psychoactive substances or potentially addictive behaviours.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, protection from harm, bullying/stereotyping/discrimination, and substances/addictive behaviours.

### C-06

Learn about physical, emotional, and social changes during puberty and adolescence and influences on identity, including sexual identity, gender, values, and beliefs.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, puberty/adolescence and influences on individual identity.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Daily participation is an explicit competency. The curriculum page does not attach a minute total to that competency or prescribe a sport, test score, body type, diet, or uniform exercise intensity. Movement learning and fitness should not be reduced to competitive performance.

Health and safety content includes puberty, identity, disease prevention, consent, and abuse prevention; these topics must not disappear from a condensed Grade 6 reference. The source's elaborations emphasize affirmative and ongoing consent, changing boundaries, the effects of power imbalances, and that people who experience abuse are not to blame. Understanding these expectations does not require disclosure of a learner's experiences, identity, or health information.

Examples of activities and health-information sources in [S1] are illustrative, not endorsements. Traditional First Peoples games are among the possible game contexts; appropriate local guidance and permissions remain necessary. The full elaborations are authoritative for terminology and scope, and this record is not emergency or clinical instruction.
