# Spanish - Grade 6

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 6](../../README.md) > [Languages](../README.md) > Spanish

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, as a second-language course option. Applicability to other programs has not been established here.

**Document language:** English, as is the official source. Spanish is the target language; this summary does not prescribe classroom language-of-instruction arrangements.

**Learning objective:** Understand and exchange familiar information in Spanish, connecting sound and written patterns with communication about people and interests and with cultural understanding.

**Prerequisites:** Earlier exposure to Spanish sounds and everyday exchanges can help; this reference sets no admission or proficiency requirement.

**Materials:** None beyond this reference and access to [S1]. Later texts or media require their own language, accessibility, attribution, and permissions review.

**How to use:** Follow the locators to the Grade 6 standards and elaborations in [S1]. Local IDs identify repository summaries, not official BC codes. Consult [S2], [S3], and the [Languages inventory](../README.md#requirement-and-course-choice) for the second-language requirement, board-selected offerings, and conditional exceptions; Spanish is not required of every learner.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Attending closely to what is heard and seen allows learners to use familiar Spanish patterns in two-way communication.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, listening/viewing with intent and reciprocal communication with high-frequency words and patterns.

### BI-02

Another language provides ways to explore identity. Stories help develop that language while opening up experiences and understandings of the world.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, identity through a new language and stories for language acquisition and understanding the world.

### BI-03

Learning about language in diverse communities broadens cultural awareness.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, language from diverse communities and cultural awareness.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Relate Spanish pronunciation, letter patterns, and punctuation to meaning, and notice what intonation and vocal expression add. Understand stories and familiar language patterns, finding key information in slow, clear speech and other texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Thinking and communicating, pronunciation/letter patterns/punctuation, intonation/tone/meaning, key information, stories, and high-frequency words and patterns.

### CC-02

Use context, known language, and non-verbal clues to support understanding. Ask for clarification and respond to straightforward questions and directions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Thinking and communicating, language-learning strategies, non-verbal cues, responses to questions/commands/instructions, and seeking clarification.

### CC-03

Exchange information and ideas orally and in writing. Select ways to present meaning that support the abilities of both the learner and the audience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Thinking and communicating, oral/written exchanges and presentation formats suited to diverse abilities.

### CC-04

Consider how culture influences personal and shared experience and differing worldviews. Recognize First Peoples perspectives and knowledge, local cultural knowledge, and other ways of knowing.

**Source locator:** [S1], Personal and social awareness, cultural lens and First Peoples perspectives and knowledge.

## Content

### C-01

Learn Spanish speech sounds and recurring written patterns that help make pronunciation predictable.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Spanish phonemes and Spanish letter patterns.

### C-02

Develop familiar language for questions and descriptions of other people, including third-person forms. Use this repertoire to communicate interests, opinions, preferences, emotions, and physical well-being.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, common high-frequency vocabulary, sentence structures, and expressions: questions, descriptions of others, hobbies/interests, opinions/preferences, and emotions/physical health.

### C-03

Learn about the cultural life of Hispanic communities in Canada and internationally, including their artistic expression and the context in which creative works are shared.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, cultural aspects of Hispanic communities across Canada and around the world and Hispanic works of art; art elaboration on appropriation and plagiarism.

### C-04

Explore First Peoples connections among language, oral histories, identity, and relationships with place.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, First Peoples perspectives connecting language and culture, including oral histories, identity, and place.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The written-pattern elaborations include recurring endings such as `-mente` and `-ción`; they clarify pronunciation rather than establish a required word list. Third-person descriptions belong to the Grade 6 scope, but this record does not prescribe a complete verb-conjugation sequence or a numerical proficiency level.

"Text" includes oral, written, visual, and digital forms. The source emphasizes key information and familiar patterns in slow, clear speech and simple texts, not unrestricted fluency. Suggested cultural contexts, artists, and presentation aids are not compulsory selections.

The source uses "Hispanic communities" for its cultural scope; this should not flatten varied national, local, or Indigenous identities into one culture. First Peoples perspectives remain a distinct part of the curriculum. Follow community protocols, copyright, and the art elaboration's concerns about appropriation and plagiarism before reusing stories or other works.
