# Career Education - Grade 6

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 6 overview](../README.md) > Career Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; shared non-language expectations under [S3], sections 1-2. See the [program overview](../README.md#choose-a-program) for instruction-language distinctions and applicable exceptions.

**Document language:** English; this does not set the language of classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Connect self-knowledge, community participation, safe and inclusive collaboration, and purposeful planning with expanding possibilities for learning and work.

**Prerequisites:** Experiences with personal goals and working with others are helpful, not formal entry requirements. Learners do not need to have chosen an occupation.

**Materials:** No special materials are needed to read the reference. It does not require a career test, public online profile, job placement, or purchase.

**How to use:** The Grade 6 page [S1] presents **Grades 6-7** standards, confirmed by the corresponding section headings in the official compilation [S2]. The target of this record is Grade 6; the source band does not create a separate Grade 6 checklist. Local IDs are repository references, not official BC standard codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Ideas about future work grow from one's sense of self and relationships with family, peers, and community. A digital presence is also part of how a person is known publicly.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, influences on attitudes toward careers and personal digital/public identity.

### BI-02

Respectful and inclusive conduct, ethical choices, and shared attention to safety support both present learning environments and later participation in work.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, workplace behaviours and shared responsibility for safe environments.

### BI-03

Leadership involves planning with others and setting goals. New experiences inside and outside school can develop skills and open possibilities that were not apparent before.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, leadership and new experiences expanding skills/options.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Connect interests, strengths, abilities, and preferences with possible career directions. Question how public identity, including digital presence and everyday communication, can have helpful or harmful consequences.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, personal preferences/skills/strengths and public identity.

### CC-02

Consider the reciprocal relationship between a person and community, including service learning and responsibility to contribute. Practise respect and inclusion in diverse settings and recognize how family, peers, and communities influence attitudes toward work.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, service learning, positive behaviours, self/community relationships, and influences on career choices.

### CC-03

Use innovative and entrepreneurial thinking to solve problems, contribute leadership in collaborative work, and apply project-management skills to career development.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, entrepreneurial/innovative thinking, leadership, and project management.

### CC-04

Set realistic goals over shorter and longer periods, identify steps toward them, and monitor progress. Recognize how trying new experiences and taking considered learning risks can broaden future options.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, learning goals and the value of new experiences, innovation, and risk-taking.

### CC-05

Demonstrate safety in experiential learning, and explore volunteering and other experiences beyond school for what they can contribute to learning and career development.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, safety skills and volunteer/out-of-school opportunities.

## Content

### C-01

Learn approaches to self-assessment, goal setting, decisions, and problem solving. Leadership and project management connect an idea with a plan, resources, action, and reflection.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Personal Development.

### C-02

Examine needs and opportunities locally and globally, including volunteering and global citizenship. Cultural and social awareness includes understanding identity and differences and honouring Indigenous traditions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community and its cultural/social awareness elaboration.

### C-03

Consider what shapes the kinds of work available in a community, how technology affects learning and work, and how mentors and personal networks contribute to decisions about future pathways.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Life and Career Plan.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The **6-7 band** is the source scope, not a rule assigning a particular topic to one of those two years. This elementary career-learning reference is not Career Life Education, Career Life Connections, a graduation plan, or a prescribed work-experience course.

Exploring volunteering does not establish a mandatory service-hours total. Considering a digital identity does not require learners to publish personal information or create social-media accounts. Self-assessment here concerns reflection and possibilities, not ranking learners or fixing them to an occupational track.

The source's elaborations connect learning with relationships, place, and generational responsibilities. Keep those First Peoples perspectives within the subject and use appropriate local guidance. Full elaborations remain at [S1] and [S2].
