# Spanish - Grade 9

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 9](../../README.md) > [Languages](../README.md) > Spanish

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; additional-language study where offered and selected. The immersion policy [S3] and the course's explicit French-program guide designation [S4] support this mapping, not compulsory enrolment or identical local offerings. This record reviews the English course source, not a French-language edition.

**Document language:** English; the reviewed official source is English with Spanish examples. Spanish is the learning target. These fields do not determine the language of classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Interpret and exchange Spanish messages, narrate connected experiences across time, and examine how cultural practices and stories contribute to identity and perspective.

**Prerequisites:** Prior Spanish study is useful preparation for this continuing-course pathway. This reference does not prescribe an admission rule; consult the school about placement and the distinct [Spanish Entry 9 option](../spanish-entry/README.md).

**Materials:** Use [S1] to consult this reference. Classroom texts, recordings, and community activities should state their own accessibility arrangements, materials, attribution, and permissions.

**How to use:** Follow the source locators and complete elaborations before mapping learning resources to local items. IDs are repository references, not official BC codes, assessment levels, or a teaching sequence.

The Grade 9 standards are distinct from other grades in a compiled download and from Spanish Entry 9. The second-language requirement in [S2] concerns Grades 5-8; the Grade 9 catalog does not require every student to take each language.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Intentional listening and viewing support Spanish comprehension, and conversation about personally meaningful topics gives learners a reason to communicate.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, listening and viewing with intent; conversing about things learners care about.

### BI-02

Learning another language can shift how learners understand their own identity. Creative expression offers ways to encounter and appreciate cultural differences.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, language acquisition and identity; creative works and cultural diversity.

### BI-03

Stories communicate experiences and viewpoints and can carry knowledge, history, and a sense of belonging.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, sharing experiences and perspectives through stories and its elaboration.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Relate Spanish letter patterns to pronunciation and meaning, and use context and other strategies to interpret texts. Identify significant information and supporting details, including when and where events occur, across speech and other forms of communication.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Thinking and communicating: Spanish letter patterns, pronunciation, and meaning; deriving meaning from speech and other texts; language-learning strategies.

### CC-02

Exchange ideas orally and in writing, checking whether meaning is shared. Use clarification and adjustments such as repetition or reformulation to resolve misunderstandings.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Thinking and communicating: exchanging ideas and information, seeking clarification, and verifying meaning.

### CC-03

Narrate in both speech and writing, using transitions and time expressions to connect past, present, and future events. Choose presentation forms that suit the learner's and others' diverse abilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Thinking and communicating: narrating stories and its time-frame and logical-progression elaborations; presentation formats suited to diverse abilities.

### CC-04

Analyze differences and similarities between personal cultural practices and those of Hispanic communities, and engage with people and communities. Examine how cultural perspectives influence individual and shared experience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Personal and social awareness: analysis of cultural practices, experiences with Hispanic people and communities, and examining experiences through a cultural lens.

### CC-05

Recognize stories as contributors to personal, family, and community identity. Attend to First Peoples perspectives and knowledge, including how stories communicate worldviews, alongside other ways of knowing and local cultural knowledge.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Personal and social awareness: story and identity with the First Peoples stories elaboration; First Peoples perspectives and knowledge, other ways of knowing, and local cultural knowledge.

## Content

### C-01

Build knowledge of recurring Spanish spelling-sound patterns to support recognition and pronunciation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Spanish letter patterns and its consistent-pronunciation examples.

### C-02

Develop common language for asking questions, describing people and situations, and expressing interests, needs, and opinions. Sequence events and adjust wording to communicate different time frames with common verbs in context.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, common vocabulary, sentence structures, and expressions: questions, descriptions, sequences, personal concerns, and past/present/future time frames.

### C-03

Understand how form, audience, register, and purpose shape a text, and how story elements connect to organize a narrative.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, elements of common texts and common elements of stories.

### C-04

Explore artistic expression and the practices, traditions, and attitudes of different Hispanic regions. Understand the ethical responsibilities involved in using cultural material and crediting its creators.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, cultural practices, traditions, and attitudes in Hispanic regions; Hispanic works of art; ethics of cultural appropriation and plagiarism.

### C-05

Consider how First Peoples oral histories and relationships to place and identity connect language with cultural knowledge.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, First Peoples perspectives connecting language and culture: oral histories, identity, and place.

## Elaborations and boundaries

This record must not be collapsed into [Spanish Entry 9](../spanish-entry/curriculum.md). The continuing Spanish source explicitly expects spoken and written narration, including logical progression and past, present, and future reference. Entry has its own supported conditions and competency list.

The content uses common verbs in context across time frames; it does not establish a complete conjugation syllabus or a standardized proficiency rating. Spelling examples such as `ll` and `rr` support recognition of patterns, not an assertion that pronunciation is identical in every Spanish-speaking region.

The source uses the term *Hispanic* for its regional and community scope. Cultural inquiry includes everyday practices and attitudes, not only festivals or a single country's traditions. First Peoples story perspectives remain explicit, with appropriate context, attribution, and permissions.

Texts may be oral, written, visual, digital, or combined, and presentation choices should account for diverse abilities. Particular stories, events, and art forms in the elaborations are examples, not a compulsory resource list. No third-party works are reproduced here.
