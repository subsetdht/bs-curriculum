## Programs, languages, and course choice

| Program | Source-checked records | Scope and remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English-language | 10 | All ten named additional-language choices; offerings and placement remain local decisions. |
| Early French Immersion | 10 | Guide-level applicability where offered and selected, supported by the program-guide order and immersion policy. Core French is an additional-course option only, not the immersion language-arts route. |
| Late French Immersion | 10 | The same guide-level scope, with the policy's common secondary provisions. Local suitability and selection remain separate; Core French cannot replace immersion language arts. |
| Francophone | 10 | The program-guide order explicitly lists French-program guides for these courses. This is optional curriculum applicability, not a mandatory course list; Core French cannot replace first-language French arts. |

The [French Immersion Program policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/french-immersion-program), reviewed 2026-09-14, requires parallel curriculum and directs Late Immersion secondary provision to the common secondary model. These labels describe course applicability when an option is selected, not a promise that every school offers it. First-language Francophone and immersion language arts remain separate from Core French.

The [Educational Program Guide Order, M231/19](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/legislation-policy/legislation/schoollaw/e/m333_99.pdf), sections 1-2, explicitly designates the K-9 course guides and French documents for French Immersion and Programme francophone. The complete K-9 schedule on pages E-25-E-26 was reviewed **2026-09-14**. It includes the nine non-Core-French guides and also lists the French Core French 5-12 guide. This establishes guide-level applicability, not a claim that Core French is a usual or required course for learners already studying in French. The legacy filename does not make this the repealed M333/99 order, and listed publication years are not newly established revisions of the live Grade 9 standards.

The [Language Education Policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/language-education-policy), reviewed 2026-09-14, describes the second-language requirement for **Grades 5-8** and board decisions about language offerings. This Grade 9 list is not a compulsory-study checklist. Local requirements and course placement should be confirmed with the school.

Read this alongside the [Required Areas of Learning Order](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/legislation-policy/legislation/schoollaw/e/m295_95.pdf) and [grade policy notes](../README.md#program-policy-and-course-selection): required areas, recognized guides, and an individual learner's course selection are different questions.
