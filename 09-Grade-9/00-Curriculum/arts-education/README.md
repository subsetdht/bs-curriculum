## Course choices and written summaries

The provincial inventory includes **Arts Education 9 itself**, as well as four discipline-specific alternatives. The [Arts Education introduction](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/arts-education/introduction), under Flexible teaching and learning and A unified curriculum, explains that Grade 9 offers unified and discipline-specific study. Learning may focus on a discipline or combine disciplines without erasing their distinct qualities.

The required Arts Education area is not a requirement for every learner to complete all five listed courses. Consult the [grade overview](../README.md) for required-area and local course-selection context. These provincial choices are not a guarantee of every school's offerings.
