# Science - Kindergarten

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Kindergarten](../README.md) > Science

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial subject expectations, supported by [S2].

**Document language:** English. The official page reviewed is also in English; this does not determine the language of instruction in the different programs.

**Learning objective:** Use curiosity and close observation to learn about nearby living things, everyday materials, movement, and changes in the natural world, while developing ways to share and apply learning.

**Prerequisites:** Familiar experiences of the surroundings are useful starting points. This reference does not set a prior science-knowledge or literacy requirement.

**Materials:** None are required to read the reference. Investigations need their own accessible materials, safety instructions, and supervision arrangements.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for the official Kindergarten expectations and elaborations. The local IDs identify this repository's summaries, not ministry codes or a prescribed sequence of experiments. The [program overview](../README.md#choose-a-program) explains how to combine this subject with the appropriate language arts.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Close observation makes the features of plants and animals available for investigation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, observable features of plants and animals.

### BI-02

Everyday encounters with familiar materials offer a way to explore matter.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, interactions with matter through familiar materials.

### BI-03

An object's characteristics help explain the ways it moves.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, motion in relation to object properties.

### BI-04

Living things experience changing conditions over a day and through the seasons.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, effects of daily and seasonal changes on living things.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Approach familiar surroundings with curiosity: notice objects and events, and ask simple questions about what is observed.

**Source locator:** [S1], Questioning and predicting, curiosity, observations in familiar contexts, and simple questions.

### CC-02

Explore using the senses, handle materials safely, and make simple measurements with non-standard units.

**Source locator:** [S1], Planning and conducting, sensory observations, safe manipulation, and non-standard measurement.

### CC-03

Experience and interpret the local environment, discuss observations, and represent observations and ideas in drawn charts and simple pictographs. Recognize First Peoples narratives, songs, and art as ways of sharing knowledge.

**Source locator:** [S1], Processing and analyzing data and information, local experience, First Peoples knowledge-sharing, discussion, charts, and pictographs.

### CC-04

Use learning in caring for oneself, family, the classroom, and the school. Try applying ideas in a new situation and contribute new or improved ideas when solving problems.

**Source locator:** [S1], Applying and innovating, caring, transfer of learning, and generating or refining ideas.

### CC-05

Share observations and ideas orally, and express and reflect on personal experiences of place.

**Source locator:** [S1], Communicating, oral sharing and reflection on place.

## Content

### C-01

Learn about the basic needs of plants and animals and the features or behaviours that help local organisms survive. The source connects these needs with habitat, including food, water, shelter, and space.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, basic needs of plants and animals and adaptations of local plants and animals; habitat and adaptation elaborations.

### C-02

Describe familiar materials through observable characteristics, such as their texture or flexibility, and notice how materials differ.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, properties of familiar materials and their elaborations.

### C-03

Explore how pushes and pulls affect movement, and how size, shape, and the material an object is made from influence the way it moves.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, effects of pushes/pulls and effects of size, shape, and materials on movement.

### C-04

Learn about changes in weather and seasons and how living things adjust to daily and seasonal cycles.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, weather changes, seasonal changes, and adjustments by living things to daily and seasonal cycles.

### C-05

Learn about local First Peoples uses of plants and animals and First Peoples knowledge of seasonal change. These are place-connected aspects of learning about living things, not a single generic account for all communities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, local First Peoples uses of plants and animals and First Peoples knowledge of seasonal changes.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The source's Kindergarten inquiry work emphasizes observation, simple questions, safe exploration, and communicating experience. Although one strand is called "Questioning and predicting," its listed Kindergarten competencies do not add Grade 1's explicit prediction expectation. There is no separate Kindergarten "Evaluating" strand on this page; do not import later-grade standards simply to fill a familiar structure.

This grade studies motion, familiar material properties, and daily and seasonal change. Grade 1's light-and-sound content is not part of this record. The official elaborations at [S1] explain terms and provide examples, not a compulsory list of species, experiments, or weather instruments.

First Peoples knowledge and knowledge-sharing are present in both competencies and content. Use appropriate local guidance, attribution, and permissions. A reference to uses of plants and animals does not authorize harvesting, tasting, or reproducing community knowledge. Any investigation requires its own safety and accessibility planning.
