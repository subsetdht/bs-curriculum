# Career Education - Kindergarten

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Kindergarten](../README.md) > Career Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone. These shared provincial expectations use the applicability established in the [Kindergarten program scope and sources](../README.md#program-scope-and-sources).

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`); the primary official source is in English (`en`). Classroom language and the programs' different language-arts pathways are addressed in the [program overview](../README.md#choose-a-program).

**Learning objective:** Develop self-knowledge, confidence, positive relationships, and purposeful learning habits while noticing how people's different roles and skills contribute to a community.

**Prerequisites:** Everyday experiences, interests, and relationships offer starting points. This reference assumes neither prior career knowledge nor independent mastery of school routines.

**Materials:** None are needed to read the reference. Future activities should state their own materials and supports without requiring private information about a family's employment, income, or circumstances.

**How to use:** Follow the locators in [S1] for official expectations and elaborations. The official compilation [S2] places this learning under **Kindergarten-Grade 3**. This record targets Kindergarten within that shared band; it does not invent a separate annual set of career standards. Local IDs are repository mapping references, not official BC standard codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Discovering personal interests and capabilities helps learners build confidence in themselves.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, self-discovery and confidence.

### BI-02

Belonging and contributing connect people to their families and communities. Different roles draw on different skills, and working toward shared purposes depends on respectful, understandable communication.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, connected communities, common goals, collaboration and communication, and varied community roles and skills.

### BI-03

Experiences of learning build skills over time, and opportunities to learn continue throughout life.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, learning developing skills and lifelong learning.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Recognize and value personal qualities, interests, skills, and accomplishments. Choose realistic learning goals and work toward achieving them.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, personal attributes and accomplishments, and setting and achieving realistic learning goals.

### CC-02

Appreciate positive relationships and share ideas, information, feelings, and knowledge with others. Cooperate respectfully and constructively toward a common purpose.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, positive relationships, sharing, and respectful collaborative work.

### CC-03

Develop useful habits for attending to work and organizing oneself in ways that fit the learner's stage of development.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, effective work habits and developmentally appropriate organizational skills.

### CC-04

Notice and appreciate people's responsibilities and contributions in families, schools, and communities. Identify basic skills involved in community jobs and recognize how continuing to learn supports life and future career possibilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, roles and responsibilities, skills used in community jobs, and the importance of learning for life and future careers.

## Content

### C-01

Learn ways to set goals and consider how to make progress toward them. Trying something unfamiliar can also reveal interests and capabilities; this is self-exploration, not an expectation to take unsafe risks.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Personal Development, goal-setting strategies and the role of risk taking in self-exploration.

### C-02

Build cultural and social awareness alongside an understanding of responsibilities at home, at school, and in the local community, including the roles and jobs of people in that community. The source's awareness elaboration connects this learning with self-identity, recognition of cultural differences, and respect for Indigenous traditions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community, cultural and social awareness and its elaboration, roles/responsibilities, and local jobs.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The **K-3** heading and corresponding standards in [S2] establish the band; the compilation's later grades do not extend this record's scope. Kindergarten examples and supports can differ from those used in Grade 1 without creating different provincial standards.

At this stage, career learning is not occupational selection, job training, or a work-placement requirement. Self-discovery, everyday contributions, relationships, and learning habits are central. The development-level qualification on organizational skills matters: the summary is not a uniform independence threshold for children entering school.

Official elaborations at [S1] give examples of goal-setting steps, sources of support, and manageable new experiences. They are illustrations, not mandatory public-speaking tasks or prescribed activities. Do not require disclosure of personal feelings, family employment, or cultural knowledge as the only way to participate.

Local roles and traditions help give the provincial expectations meaning, but no single family structure, occupation list, or community practice is prescribed. Respect community guidance and permissions when considering Indigenous traditions.
