# Career Education - Grade 3

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 3](../README.md) > Career Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial expectations. See the [program overview](../README.md#choose-a-program) for applicability and language-of-instruction boundaries.

**Document language:** English; the official sources reviewed are in English.

**Learning objective:** Build self-knowledge, achievable learning goals, respectful working relationships, and understanding of the varied contributions people make in a community.

**Prerequisites:** Everyday experiences of learning and cooperation provide entry points. No employment experience or decision about a future occupation is expected.

**Materials:** None are required for this reference. Activities should not depend on disclosure of family employment, finances, or other private information.

**How to use:** [S1] is the Grade 3 view of the **Kindergarten-Grade 3 band**, confirmed by the corresponding section in the official compilation [S2]. The record applies that shared band, not a newly invented Grade 3 progression. Local IDs are repository references, not ministry codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Discovering one's qualities, interests, and abilities can build confidence.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, self-discovery and confidence.

### BI-02

Communities benefit from connected people with varied skills and roles. Respectful, clear communication makes work toward shared goals more effective.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, community connections, collaboration, and varied roles and skills.

### BI-03

Learning contributes to skill development across experiences and continues throughout life.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, learning developing skills and lifelong learning.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Recognize and value personal attributes, interests, skills, and accomplishments. Set attainable learning goals and work toward achieving them.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, personal attributes/skills/interests/accomplishments and realistic learning goals.

### CC-02

Recognize the value of positive relationships, share ideas and feelings appropriately, and contribute respectfully to collaborative work with a common purpose.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, positive relationships, sharing, and respectful/constructive collaboration.

### CC-03

Use effective work and organization habits suited to the learner's development, rather than treating successful learning as dependent only on a finished product.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, developmentally appropriate work habits and organizational skills.

### CC-04

Appreciate people's responsibilities in families, schools, and communities. Recognize basic skills used in local jobs and connect the importance of present learning with future possibilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, community roles/responsibilities, job skills, and learning in life and future careers.

## Content

### C-01

Learn strategies for setting goals and understand how trying something unfamiliar can contribute to self-discovery.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Personal Development: goal-setting strategies and risk taking in self-exploration.

### C-02

Develop cultural and social awareness in relation to self and community. The elaboration includes recognizing cultural differences and honouring Indigenous traditions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community: cultural and social awareness and its elaboration.

### C-03

Learn about responsibilities and roles at home, at school, and in the local community, including jobs and the skills that help people contribute.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community: roles/responsibilities and jobs in the local community.

## Elaborations and boundaries

These are K-3 standards, even though the page is reached through the Grade 3 selector. The later bands in [S2] are not Grade 3 requirements: do not import a digital-identity study, project-management syllabus, or formal career plan from them.

Self-exploration through "risk taking" means trying an unfamiliar but appropriate experience, not unsafe behaviour. The curriculum does not require children to choose a career, compare family income, or disclose personal circumstances.

Official examples remain at [S1]. Cultural awareness is not permission to reproduce community traditions or to assume that one account represents every Indigenous community.
