### Early French Immersion

Use [French Immersion Language Arts 3](french-immersion-language-arts/curriculum.md) and the seven shared subjects. This pathway contains eight records; the language-arts source is French and the local summary is English.

Grade 3 English Language Arts is **conditional on local scheduling**, not a provincial requirement for every immersion learner. The provincial policy requires ELA from Grade 4 and allows recommended language allocations with a Grade 3 or Grade 4 transition. The reviewed SD43 sources do not settle its Grade 3 schedule. See [program context](program-context.md#grade-3-english-language-arts); do not infer Grade 3 delivery from SD43's K-2 statement.

### Francophone

Use [Francophone Language Arts 3](francophone-language-arts/curriculum.md) and the seven shared subjects. This pathway contains eight records. First-language French is a distinct curriculum, not a translation of immersion language arts.

Shared-subject applicability follows provincial ministerial orders, not the language of a translated page. CSF's reviewed provider guidance places first-language English courses from Grade 4; that is separate from province-wide immersion policy. See the [source-backed program context](program-context.md) for the authority, French-guide provenance, and limitations.

All program pathways lead to English-language reference summaries. They do not prescribe English classroom instruction or provide French-language teaching materials. No Grade 3 Late French Immersion pathway is established by the reviewed sources.

## Using the references

Read Scope and use before mapping learning material. **Big Ideas** summarize enduring understandings, **Curricular Competencies** describe skills and processes, and **Content** identifies knowledge. Every item has a source locator and stable local ID.

For example, [Mathematics 3: C-04](mathematics/curriculum.md#c-04) has the canonical repository key `bc-g03-mathematics#c-04`. These are not official BC standard codes, source item numbers, or a suggested teaching order. Follow the [reference convention](../../curriculum-format.md#linking-to-records-and-items).

No special materials or formal prerequisites are needed to read these documents. Future activities must state their own materials, accessibility, prerequisites, program suitability, and language. Individual educational programs may differ under the [specified provincial exceptions](program-context.md#individual-program-boundaries).

## Learning across subjects

The [BC curriculum model](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/overview) connects knowing, doing, and understanding. [Core Competencies](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/competencies) develop across areas of learning rather than replacing subject-specific competencies.

Career Education uses a verified K-3 band. ADST also uses K-3 competencies, but explicitly combines them with Grade 3 content from other subjects instead of supplying its own content list. All other records here have Grade 3 source scope, even when a download contains multiple grades.

First Peoples perspectives remain within the relevant subjects, including Grade 3 Social Studies' global Indigenous focus and the Francophone curriculum's identity, oral-tradition, and narrative strands. Work with appropriate sources and community guidance; a curriculum reference does not grant permission to copy cultural works.

## Coverage and limits

These ten records are **core summaries**, not exhaustive transcripts or elaboration inventories. All have actual review dates and `source.version: null` because a revision of the current subject source was not established. The review date is not a curriculum edition or ministry endorsement.

The Francophone language-arts record documents a [live-page/DOCX spelling-elaboration discrepancy](francophone-language-arts/curriculum.md#elaborations-and-boundaries), following the live page without inventing a revision history. No subject-source access blocker remains.

Program applicability is established for the three pathways above, but SD43 Grade 3 immersion ELA timing and language allocation remain unconfirmed in the reviewed local sources. School timetables, admission, and individual placement are not determined here. No unsupported program or local schedule is counted as completed coverage.

French-language editions of these summaries, lessons, assessments, resources, and tools have not been authored. The [resources](../01-Resources), [tools](../02-Tools), and [additional-content](../03-Additional-Content) folders remain empty. Continue to maintain the existing landings using the [landing-page workflow](../../curriculum-format.md#filling-a-landing-page).
