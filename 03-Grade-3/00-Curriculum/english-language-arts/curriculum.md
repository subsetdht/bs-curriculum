# English Language Arts - Grade 3

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 3](../README.md) > English Language Arts

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language. This record does not establish an English Language Arts timetable for immersion or Francophone schools; see the [program overview](../README.md#choose-a-program).

**Document language:** English; the official curriculum source is in English.

**Learning objective:** Develop fluent, thoughtful reading and effective communication across oral, written, visual, and digital texts, connecting language with identity, community, and different perspectives.

**Prerequisites:** Experience with conversation, reading, writing, and representing ideas offers starting points, not an admission test or a fixed commercial reading level.

**Materials:** None are required for this reference. Text selections and later activities need their own suitability, accessibility, and rights review.

**How to use:** Read alongside [S1] and follow the item locators for official wording and elaborations. Repository IDs are not official BC standard codes. Keep this curriculum distinct from [French Immersion Language Arts](../french-immersion-language-arts/README.md) and [Francophone Language Arts](../francophone-language-arts/README.md).

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Playful, imaginative encounters with language can bring enjoyment and help learners discover how communication works.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, creativity/joy and creative play with language.

### BI-02

Stories and other texts offer ways to understand personal, family, and community experience, while allowing more than one interpretation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, learning about self/family/community and different perspectives on stories.

### BI-03

Wondering and asking questions can open new understandings of oneself and the wider world.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, curiosity and discovery.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Read Grade 3 texts fluently and for meaning. Bring together prior knowledge and information from different sources, using suitable reading, listening, and viewing strategies to extend understanding.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Comprehend and connect: grade-level fluency, information sources, prior knowledge, connections, and comprehension strategies.

### CC-02

Participate actively as a reader, viewer, and listener. Recognize texts' differing purposes and story structures, make meaningful personal connections, and explain how stories contribute to identity in families and communities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Comprehend and connect: text purposes, active engagement, story/identity, personal connections, and story elements.

### CC-03

Recognize how stories in First Peoples cultures connect people with family and community, and develop understanding of their connections with land.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Comprehend and connect: First Peoples stories, family/community, and land.

### CC-04

Build shared understanding by exchanging ideas and perspectives. Plan and create varied communications for particular purposes and audiences, including texts that deepen awareness of self, family, and community.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Create and communicate: exchange of perspectives, creation of texts, and communication forms/purposes/audiences.

### CC-05

Express ideas in sentences with most Canadian spelling, grammar, and punctuation conventions. Expand and apply knowledge of words and how they are formed.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Create and communicate: sentence conventions and expanding word knowledge.

### CC-06

Explore and appreciate First Peoples oral traditions and use oral storytelling processes. Preparation, telling, and expressive delivery matter alongside written composition.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Create and communicate: First Peoples oral traditions and oral storytelling processes.

## Content

### C-01

Connect a story's characters and setting with its plot, conflict, and theme to understand how its parts create meaning. Explore the functions and genres of stories and other texts, including features that organize information for readers.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Story/text: story elements, functions/genres, and text features.

### C-02

Learn how literary choices create effects, including descriptive or figurative language and patterns of sound or imagery. The elaborations connect these choices with such features as simile, rhythm, rhyme, and alliteration.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Story/text: literary elements and devices, with elaboration.

### C-03

Develop reading strategies that connect word recognition and decoding with comprehension and self-monitoring. In spoken exchanges, listen for relevant information, clarify uncertainty, express ideas coherently, and attend to the audience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Strategies and processes: reading strategies and oral language strategies.

### C-04

Use reflection and goal setting to understand one's learning as a reader and writer. Develop writing through a process that can include revising, editing, and considering the audience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Strategies and processes: metacognitive strategies and writing processes.

### C-05

Notice how voice and gesture affect oral communication, and how word patterns and word families support language knowledge.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions: features of oral language, word patterns, and word families.

### C-06

Develop readable handwriting with word spacing, compound sentence structure, and written conventions. The convention elaboration includes sentence punctuation and apostrophes in contractions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions: legible handwriting, sentence structure, and conventions.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The source's term "text" includes spoken, printed, visual, and digital communication, not only books. Its fluency elaboration includes comprehension, phrasing, and punctuation; this summary adds no words-per-minute threshold, prescribed reading scheme, or universal pace.

The official reading-strategy elaborations remain at [S1]. This is a curriculum reference, not an endorsement of a particular literacy intervention or a requirement to use every example as a teaching method.

Grade 3 includes compound sentences and developing command of most conventions; do not import a later-grade essay format or grammar inventory. Oral tradition and storytelling also remain part of learning. Permission and community protocols govern access to and retelling of First Peoples stories.

English-language document authorship does not make this a French language-arts curriculum. Provincial and local scheduling distinctions for Grade 3 are recorded separately in the program overview.
