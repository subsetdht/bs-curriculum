# French Immersion Language Arts - Grade 1

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](../README.md) > French Immersion Language Arts

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** Early French Immersion. This is distinct from English Language Arts, Core French, and the first-language Francophone program.

**Document language:** English; the authoritative source is in French. This is an original summary, not an authorized translation or French classroom resource. Source locators below are English descriptions of the French sections and topics.

**Learning objective:** Develop the ability to understand and communicate in French through familiar interactions, reading, and early writing, while engaging with language and culture.

**Prerequisites:** Prior immersion experiences may help learners; this reference does not set entry or language-proficiency criteria. Consult the district for program placement.

**Materials:** No special materials are required to read this reference. Classroom texts and activities should be appropriate for French-language learning and separately attributed.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for official French wording and elaborations, and pair this record with the shared subjects in the [Grade 1 overview](../README.md). Local IDs are repository references, not official BC standard codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Using French can help learners connect with Francophone communities. Willingness to try unfamiliar language supports growing communication skills.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas (English gloss), Francophone belonging and risk-taking in a new language.

### BI-02

A growing vocabulary gives learners more ways to express what they mean.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas (English gloss), vocabulary and being understood.

### BI-03

Reading brings together word decoding and understanding. Recognizing how different kinds of texts are organized supports that understanding.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas (English gloss), decoding/meaning and text structures.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Explore and interpret texts, including Francophone and Indigenous cultural references. Recognize familiar words, use meaningful word groups and text features, identify story elements, and distinguish information texts from narratives. Make predictions and connections, understand the overall message, and retell it in one's own words.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies (English gloss), Explore and reflect: cultural elements, word recognition, comprehension, story elements, and text types.

### CC-02

Speak about oneself and everyday experiences, interact respectfully with different listeners, and take initiative in asking questions. Use familiar simple sentence patterns to communicate orally.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies (English gloss), Create and communicate: everyday speech, respectful interaction, questions, and oral sentence structures.

### CC-03

Read simple sentences using decoding, clear articulation, and attention to punctuation. Write simple sentences using words and patterns that follow the relevant spelling and sentence conventions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies (English gloss), Create and communicate: reading and writing simple sentences.

## Content

### C-01

Learn ways to take part in conversation, including attentive listening, turn-taking, greetings, and polite expressions, alongside cultural elements encountered in French.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), communication strategies and cultural elements.

### C-02

Recognize characters, settings, and events in stories. Learn how stories and information texts are organized and how text features help readers.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), story elements and textual organization.

### C-03

Develop awareness of French speech sounds, syllables, and rhyme. Learn written distinctions such as uppercase/lowercase letters, spaces between words, and punctuation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), phonological awareness and written conventions.

### C-04

Use basic language tools, including adjectives, simple affirmative sentences, questions formed with "est-ce que", and present-tense action verbs.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), language tools.

## Elaborations and boundaries

These summaries describe learning French as an additional language. Do not import Grade 1 English spelling conventions or replace this curriculum with English Language Arts taught through translation. The separate Francophone curriculum serves a different program.

The French source contains the precise terminology and elaborations. This record does not supply a required vocabulary list, pronunciation program, or fixed sequence of texts. Cultural references require appropriate attribution and permissions; no literary or community works are reproduced.
