# Physical and Health Education - Grade 1

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](../README.md) > Physical and Health Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial subject expectations under [S2], sections 1-2.

**Document language:** English, separately from the language of classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Develop movement skills, healthy choices, personal safety, caring relationships, and awareness of physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

**Prerequisites:** Build from each learner's existing movement and communication experiences. This reference does not set a fitness threshold or assume a particular ability.

**Materials:** None are needed to read the reference. Activities require their own accessible materials, supervision, safety arrangements, and adaptations.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for official expectations, elaborations, and relevant supports. Local IDs help map resources; they are not official standard codes or individual health assessments.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Regular activity builds movement confidence and physical literacy while contributing to healthy living.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, daily activity, movement skills, and physical literacy.

### BI-02

Understanding oneself, other people, and the body supports caring relationships and health-related choices. Well-being includes mental and emotional health as well as physical health.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, self/others and relationships, body knowledge and choices, and the dimensions of health.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Practise and demonstrate fundamental movements across activities and environments, noticing how the body responds. Participate with attention to safety, fairness, and leadership.

**Source locator:** [S1], Physical literacy, movement skills, body responses, and safe/fair participation.

### CC-02

Participate each day in moderate-to-vigorous physical activity and identify opportunities to be active in different settings. Explore foods, health-supporting choices, and basic health information.

**Source locator:** [S1], Healthy and active living, daily participation, activity opportunities, food, choices, and information sources.

### CC-03

Describe how to avoid or respond to unsafe or uncomfortable situations. Show respect in shared activities and recognize caring actions in classrooms and families.

**Source locator:** [S1], Social and community health, safety responses, respectful behaviour, and caring behaviours.

### CC-04

Recognize practices that support mental well-being, communicate about feelings and worries, and identify personal interests, preferences, and skills.

**Source locator:** [S1], Mental well-being, supportive practices, feelings/worries, and self-knowledge.

## Content

### C-01

Learn techniques for moving in place, travelling, and handling objects, and ways to participate in individual, paired, rhythmic, and game activities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, non-locomotor/locomotor/manipulative skills and types of physical activity.

### C-02

Understand links among food, hydration, activity, and health, including how different activities affect the body and which everyday practices support well-being.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, food/hydration, activity effects, and health-promoting practices.

### C-03

Know names for body parts, including private parts, and distinguish appropriate from inappropriate touch.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, body-part names, including male and female private parts, and ways of being touched.

### C-04

Learn about substances and their safe use or avoidance, hazards and unsafe situations, and reliable places to obtain health information.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, substances, hazards, and reliable health information.

### C-05

Recognize caring actions in groups and families and explore emotions, what can cause them, and how they affect people.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, caring behaviours and emotions.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The source includes body safety and mental well-being, not only exercise. Keep those parts visible and use accurate, inclusive, developmentally appropriate language.

Official elaborations and supports remain at [S1]. The daily-activity statement above does not add a duration, fitness score, or medical recommendation not established by this source. Learner activities need appropriate accessibility and safety planning, and should never require a child to disclose personal health information or unsafe experiences.
