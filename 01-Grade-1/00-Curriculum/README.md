# Grade 1 curriculum

**Jurisdiction:** British Columbia, Canada. **Programs covered:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone. **Document language:** English (`en-CA`). **Source review date:** 2026-09-14.

These references help families and educators locate Grade 1 expectations and connect future learning resources to them. They contain original, condensed summaries of the provincial curriculum, not official wording, a teaching schedule, or an assessment checklist. Read the linked sources for the complete expectations and elaborations.

### English-language program

Start with [English Language Arts](english-language-arts/curriculum.md) and the seven shared subjects in the [subject table](#subjects-and-learning-standards). This pathway contains eight records.

### Early French Immersion

Start with [French Immersion Language Arts](french-immersion-language-arts/curriculum.md) and the same seven shared subjects in the [subject table](#subjects-and-learning-standards). This pathway also contains eight records; the immersion language-arts source is in French and the repository summary is in English.

Shared expectations do not mean shared classroom language. The [provincial French Immersion policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/french-immersion-program) describes a parallel curriculum and French instruction in the early years. [SD43's program guidance](sd43-context.md) identifies Kindergarten through Grade 2 Early Immersion as fully French. Do not use the English Language Arts record as a substitute for the Grade 1 immersion language-arts curriculum.

### Francophone

Start with [Francophone Language Arts](francophone-language-arts/curriculum.md) and the seven shared subjects in the [subject table](#subjects-and-learning-standards). This pathway contains eight records. It serves the French first-language program, not French Immersion or English Language Arts taught in translation.

The [Required Areas of Learning Order][required-areas], sections 1-2, explicitly includes Francophone students and identifies the seven shared areas, with French Language Arts for this program. The [provincial program comparison][program-comparison] explains French-language instruction and the role of the Conseil scolaire francophone de la Colombie-Britannique (SD93). Confirm local arrangements with the responsible school authority; the separate SD43 context does not establish Francophone delivery.

All Grade 1 summaries are in English. French Immersion Language Arts and Francophone Language Arts use French sources (`fr`); the other eight records use English sources (`en`). Source language, summary language, and classroom language are distinct. French learning resources have not yet been added.

## How to use these references

Start with the appropriate program row, read a subject's Scope and use section, and follow its source locators to the official expectations. The three main curriculum sections are **Big Ideas** (understandings), **Curricular Competencies** (skills and processes), and **Content** (knowledge).

Each summary item has a stable local ID. For example, [Mathematics 1: C-01](mathematics/curriculum.md#c-01) has the complete reference `bc-g01-mathematics#c-01`. These are repository IDs, not ministry standard codes or a suggested teaching order. Use the descriptive linked label when citing an item outside its record, as specified in the [linking convention](../../curriculum-format.md#linking-to-records-and-items).

There are no special materials or formal prerequisites for reading these references. For learner activities, choose developmentally appropriate entry points and describe any materials or prerequisites in the activity itself. A grade label is not a rigid rule about a child's pace of learning.

## Learning across subjects

The [BC curriculum model](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/overview) connects understanding, doing, and knowing. The cross-curricular [Core Competencies](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/competencies) include Communication, Thinking, and Personal and Social development. They belong throughout learning, not in place of subject-specific competencies or as another isolated Grade 1 subject.

First Peoples perspectives occur within several subjects' learning standards. Keep them attached to those expectations. Use appropriate local relationships, attribution, and permissions; a curriculum reference is not permission to reproduce a community's stories, designs, or other cultural works.

Career Education and ADST both use K-3 expectations for this grade. ADST is also a useful example of integrated learning: its competencies are used with Grade 1 content from other areas. Its record explains this rather than inventing a separate Grade 1 technology-content checklist.

## District context

[SD43 / Coquitlam context](sd43-context.md) explains the local program distinction and links to current district guidance. It is separate from the BC subject records so families in other districts can use the same provincial references without adopting SD43-specific arrangements.

School timetables, term plans, resource choices, and local program availability must be confirmed with the relevant school or district. This collection does not establish those arrangements.

## Coverage and limits

The ten records provide **core summaries**, not copies of every learning standard or elaboration. Primary curriculum revision dates were not established; the review date above is not a curriculum edition. These program pathways do not determine individual placement, accommodations, or teaching schedules.

Each covered program has eight subject records: its distinct language-arts curriculum and seven shared subjects. Late French Immersion is not a Grade 1 program; Core French is not the immersion or Francophone language-arts curriculum.

Use the [central index](../../curriculum-index.md) for current coverage in other grades. French-language editions of these summaries, lessons, assessments, and classroom tools have not yet been populated. The existing [resources](../01-Resources), [tools](../02-Tools), and [additional content](../03-Additional-Content) categories remain available for later work.

The [format guide](../../curriculum-format.md) documents the reusable schema, program labels, and [landing-page workflow](../../curriculum-format.md#filling-a-landing-page). Maintain the existing landings and stable item references when reviewing or extending these summaries.

## Sources and rights

The subject pages identify their primary BC sources and individual source locators. The curriculum model, Core Competencies, provincial French Immersion policy, and SD43 program guidance were also checked on **2026-09-14**.

The [Required Areas of Learning Order][required-areas], sections 1-2, establishes the common provincial areas and the language-arts distinction, including for Francophone students. The [provincial comparison of French programs][program-comparison] supports the program and language-of-instruction distinctions. Both were reviewed on **2026-09-14**; neither turns a summary's document language into a classroom-language requirement.

BC source material remains subject to the [province's copyright policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/copyright). This repository supplies original summaries and links, not mirrored government documents or district materials.
