# Applied Design, Skills, and Technologies - Grade 1

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](../README.md) > Applied Design, Skills, and Technologies

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language and Early French Immersion; shared provincial expectations.

**Document language:** English, separately from the language of classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Develop design thinking and making through curiosity, play, safe use of tools, experimentation, and sharing.

**Prerequisites:** Exploration and play offer entry points. This reference does not require prior coding, device use, or specialist making skills.

**Materials:** None are required to read the reference. A making activity should specify its own accessible materials, suitable tools, and safety arrangements.

**How to use:** The Grade 1 view in [S1] explicitly applies **K-3 ADST competencies with grade-level content from other learning areas**. Use this record with the relevant Grade 1 subject, rather than as a separate content syllabus. Local IDs are repository references, not ministry codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Curiosity can lead to designs, while play provides opportunities to develop the skills needed to make them.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, curiosity in design and skill development through play.

### BI-02

Tools and technologies can extend what people are able to do.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, technologies and human capabilities.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Notice needs or possibilities through exploration, draw on interests and experiences to generate ideas, build on others' contributions, and select an idea to pursue.

**Source locator:** [S1], Applied Design, Ideating.

### CC-02

Select materials and tools, make a product using familiar or demonstrated approaches, and improve it through trying, noticing problems, and incorporating new ideas.

**Source locator:** [S1], Applied Design, Making.

### CC-03

Choose an audience and way to share a product. Explain its development and possible contribution, evaluate the design using personal preferences, and reflect on working alone and with others.

**Source locator:** [S1], Applied Design, Sharing: audience, demonstration, contribution, evaluation, and collaboration.

### CC-04

Use materials and technologies safely in physical and digital settings, and build skills through play and shared work.

**Source locator:** [S1], Applied Skills.

### CC-05

Explore accessible, simple tools and technologies as ways of expanding one's capabilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Applied Technologies.

## Content

### C-01

There is no separate Grade 1 ADST content list in this source. Combine the K-3 design-and-making competencies with Grade 1 content selected from another area of learning. The partner subject supplies the content expectation; ADST supplies the design, skill, and technology learning.

**Source locator:** [S1], introductory instruction to combine ADST K-3 Curricular Competencies with grade-level content from other areas.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Keep the Content heading to make this integration rule visible. Do not invent a required Grade 1 coding unit, software package, device, or standalone technology topic. If a future activity joins ADST with science or mathematics, cite the specific expectation from each subject.

Official elaborations remain at [S1]. Their examples do not establish a required product or material list. "Technology" here is not restricted to computers or screens, and making activities need separate safety and accessibility planning.
