# Career Education - Grade 1

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](../README.md) > Career Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial subject expectations under [S3], sections 1-2.

**Document language:** English, separately from the language of classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Build self-awareness, constructive learning and collaboration habits, and understanding of how people contribute through different roles in a community.

**Prerequisites:** Familiar routines and experiences with other people provide starting points. No work experience or career choice is expected.

**Materials:** None are required for this reference. Activities should state any materials and should not require private information about family employment or finances.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for the official expectations and elaborations. The ministry's compilation [S2] identifies these as **K-3 standards**; this record applies that band to Grade 1 rather than inventing a separate Grade 1-only set. Use local IDs for curriculum mapping; they are repository references, not ministry standard codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Getting to know one's interests and abilities can strengthen confidence.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, self-discovery and confidence.

### BI-02

Communities depend on connections, shared goals, respectful communication, and people contributing different skills through different roles.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, connected communities, effective collaboration, and varied roles and skills.

### BI-03

Learning contributes to skill development throughout life, rather than ending with a single task or school year.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, learning and skills and lifelong learning.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Recognize personal qualities, interests, skills, and accomplishments, and set realistic learning goals that can be worked toward and achieved.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, self-knowledge and realistic learning goals.

### CC-02

Value positive relationships, share ideas and feelings appropriately, and work respectfully with other people toward a common purpose.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, relationships, sharing, and constructive collaboration.

### CC-03

Develop work habits and ways of organizing oneself that are suitable for the learner's stage of development.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, work habits and organizational skills.

### CC-04

Recognize contributions and responsibilities in families, schools, and communities. Notice skills involved in community jobs and connect ongoing learning with future possibilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, community roles, job skills, and learning for future careers.

## Content

### C-01

Learn ways to set goals and consider how trying something unfamiliar can contribute to self-discovery.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Personal Development: goal-setting strategies and risk-taking in self-exploration.

### C-02

Develop cultural and social awareness while learning about responsibilities and roles at home, school, and in the local community, including community jobs.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community: awareness, roles/responsibilities, and jobs.

## Elaborations and boundaries

At this level, career learning concerns everyday identity, relationships, contributions, and learning habits; it is not a requirement to choose an occupation. "Risk-taking" in the content should not be read as unsafe behaviour.

The primary source is the ministry's Grade 1 view of the K-3 expectations. The compilation [S2] also contains later grade bands; use its K-3 section for this record, not expectations from those later bands. Official examples and elaborations remain at [S1].
