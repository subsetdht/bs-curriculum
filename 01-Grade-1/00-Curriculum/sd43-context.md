# Grade 1: SD43 program context

[Curriculum index](../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](README.md) > SD43 context

**District:** School District No. 43 (Coquitlam), British Columbia. **Applies to:** Grade 1 English-language and Early French Immersion pathways. **Document language:** English. **Sources checked:** 2026-09-14.

This is local implementation guidance, not an additional set of provincial learning standards. Its purpose is to help readers choose the appropriate Grade 1 records. No special materials are needed; use the linked district pages to confirm arrangements for a particular school.

## BC curriculum remains the reference

The provincial French Immersion policy describes immersion as an alternative delivery of a curriculum parallel to the English-language program, with additional-language learning through French. SD43's Early Immersion description likewise refers to completing the prescribed BC curriculum while learning French.

The shared subject records therefore describe provincial expectations. SD43-specific program delivery belongs on this page, not inside those expectations. Families outside SD43 should consult their own district for local arrangements.

## Grade 1 program distinction

| Topic | What the cited sources establish | Repository treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early French Immersion | SD43 identifies Kindergarten through Grade 2 instruction as 100% French; provincial policy likewise describes the early years as fully French | The Grade 1 immersion pathway uses French Immersion Language Arts plus shared subject expectations |
| English Language Arts | Provincial policy introduces English Language Arts after the initial immersion years, requiring it beginning in Grade 4 | The Grade 1 English Language Arts record is labelled `english-language`, not `early-french-immersion` |
| Late French Immersion | SD43 describes entry beginning in Grade 6 | No Grade 1 records are labelled `late-french-immersion` |
| Core French | SD43 distinguishes this subject from immersion and describes second-language instruction in Grades 5-8 | Do not equate Core French with Grade 1 French Immersion Language Arts |
| Francophone education | Provincial policy distinguishes first-language Francophone education from second-language immersion | Do not treat immersion records as Francophone language-arts records |

English-language summaries of shared subjects are reference material for adults. They do not change the language of instruction in an immersion class or establish that an English worksheet is appropriate there.

This page does not determine Grade 3 scheduling, entry eligibility, placement, or a particular school's offerings. The cited district pages and the school should resolve those questions; do not extrapolate from these Grade 1 references.

## Local planning is not a new requirement

Teachers may integrate subjects and choose local contexts and resources. A school newsletter, term plan, particular book, or suggested activity is not by itself evidence of a BC-wide standard.

For place-based learning, use the relevant community's guidance and permissions rather than assuming that one district example represents all First Peoples communities. The subject records retain the relevant provincial expectations without reproducing cultural works.

The district's Programs of Choice page is useful for program and registration information, but its annual dates are not curriculum standards. Follow the live page rather than treating a date in this repository as an application deadline.
