# Science - Grade 1

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](../README.md) > Science

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial subject expectations under [S2], sections 1-2.

**Document language:** English, separately from the language of classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Investigate familiar living things, materials, light, sound, and local natural patterns through observation, questions, safe exploration, and communication.

**Prerequisites:** Experiences noticing and discussing the surroundings can provide an entry point; no formal prerequisite is asserted.

**Materials:** None are required to read this reference. Investigations need their own materials, accessibility arrangements, supervision, and safety instructions.

**How to use:** Read the summaries alongside [S1]. Follow the source locators for the relevant expectations, and use the local IDs to map future resources rather than treating them as ministry codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Features and behaviours of organisms can help explain how they live successfully in their surroundings.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, survival of living things in their environment.

### BI-02

The properties of a material help determine what it can be used for.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, properties and usefulness of matter.

### BI-03

Light and sound have sources, and their observable properties can change.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, production and changing properties of light and sound.

### BI-04

Looking closely at the nearby land and sky reveals recurring patterns and cycles.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, local sky and landscape patterns.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Bring curiosity to familiar objects and events: observe, ask investigable questions, and suggest what might happen.

**Source locator:** [S1], Questioning and predicting, curiosity, observation, questions, and simple predictions.

### CC-02

Carry out safe explorations with materials, recording observations and simple measurements made with informal or non-standard methods.

**Source locator:** [S1], Planning and conducting, observations, safe testing, and simple measurements.

### CC-03

Interpret experiences of the local environment and recognize stories, songs, and art as ways First Peoples share knowledge. Organize information with drawings, pictographs, and supplied tables; look for patterns and compare observations with predictions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Processing and analyzing data and information, local experience, First Peoples knowledge-sharing, classification, comparison, and patterns.

### CC-04

Compare findings with those of others and consider how one's actions may affect the environment.

**Source locator:** [S1], Evaluating, comparing observations and environmental consequences.

### CC-05

Use learning in new situations, contribute to care for people and shared spaces, and propose or improve ideas when solving problems.

**Source locator:** [S1], Applying and innovating, caring, transfer, and new or refined ideas.

### CC-06

Share observations and thinking through speech, writing, drawing, or role-play, and reflect on personal experiences of place.

**Source locator:** [S1], Communicating, communicating observations and reflecting on place.

## Content

### C-01

Distinguish living from non-living things. Learn about local plants and animals, including their names, physical features, and animal behaviours that support life in the local environment.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, classification, local species, structural features, and behavioural adaptations.

### C-02

Connect particular material properties with different possible uses.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, material properties and uses.

### C-03

Investigate natural and human-made sources of light and sound, and how what is observed depends on the source and interacting objects.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, sources and properties of light and sound.

### C-04

Recognize familiar objects and patterns in the sky and on Earth. Include First Peoples knowledge about the sky, local landscape, plants and animals, and seasonal rounds.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, sky objects, First Peoples knowledge, and local Earth/sky patterns.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The official elaborations explain terms and give possible contexts; they are linked at [S1] rather than reproduced. This reference does not prescribe a particular experiment, equipment set, or a universal list of local species.

First Peoples knowledge is part of the curriculum, not optional enrichment to detach from it. Work with appropriate local guidance and permissions rather than inventing a generic story or seasonal practice. Activities involving light, sound, living things, or outdoor exploration need separate safety and accessibility planning.
