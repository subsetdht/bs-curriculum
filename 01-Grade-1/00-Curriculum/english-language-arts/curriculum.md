# English Language Arts - Grade 1

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](../README.md) > English Language Arts

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language. This is not the language-arts record for Grade 1 Early French Immersion.

**Document language:** English.

**Learning objective:** Build understanding and expression through reading, listening, viewing, writing, speaking, and representing, while connecting language and stories with identity and community.

**Prerequisites:** Experiences with shared stories, conversation, sounds, and print are helpful starting points, not formal admission criteria.

**Materials:** No special materials are required to read this reference. Any later reading selection or activity needs its own suitability, accessibility, and rights review.

**How to use:** Use [S1] for official expectations and elaborations. Local IDs connect future resources to this summary, not to an invented ministry coding system. Consult the [program overview](../README.md) before selecting a language-arts record.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Exploring words, stories, and questions can make language learning imaginative and enjoyable while helping children discover how communication works.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, creativity and joy, playing with language, and curiosity and wonder.

### BI-02

Stories help people understand their families and themselves, while making room for each person's distinct experiences.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, learning about self and family and everyone's unique story.

### BI-03

People share meaning through images and words, and build connections by speaking and listening to one another.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, sharing texts through pictures and words and connecting through listening and speaking.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Read Grade 1 texts fluently and for meaning. Draw on prior knowledge, information sources, appropriate reading/listening/viewing strategies, and foundational understandings of print, spoken language, and images.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, grade-level fluency, sources and prior knowledge, strategies, and foundational concepts.

### CC-02

Participate actively in reading, listening, and viewing. Connect stories with personal experience and community identity, and recognize how a story is organized and what its main elements contribute.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, active engagement, identity, personal connections, and story structure and elements.

### CC-03

Recognize that stories in First Peoples cultures connect people with family and community.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, story in First Peoples cultures.

### CC-04

Build understanding through exchanges of ideas and perspectives. Organize and present ideas in different forms, choosing ways to communicate that suit a purpose and an audience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Create and communicate, exchanging ideas, organizing/presenting, and forms for different purposes and audiences.

### CC-05

Create stories and other communications about self, family, and community. Use letters and words with developing command of Canadian spelling, grammar, and punctuation, and explore how oral stories are prepared and shared.

**Source locator:** [S1], Create and communicate, creating texts, written conventions, and oral storytelling processes.

## Content

### C-01

Learn the parts of stories, ways authors and artists create effects, and vocabulary for discussing books and other texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Story/text: story elements, literary elements/devices, and vocabulary.

### C-02

Develop strategies for reading and spoken communication, ways to think about one's own learning, and processes for creating and improving writing.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Strategies and processes: reading, oral language, metacognition, and writing processes.

### C-03

Understand how print carries meaning and relates to speech. Develop awareness of words' sounds, including phonological and phonemic awareness.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions: concepts of print, print awareness, and sound awareness.

### C-04

Develop letter formation and an understanding of simple sentence structure and written conventions, including the use of capitals and punctuation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions: letter formation, sentence structure, and conventions.

## Elaborations and boundaries

"Text" in the official curriculum is broader than a printed book: spoken, written, visual, and digital forms matter. Grade-level fluency is an expectation in [S1], but this reference does not assign a commercial reading level, book list, or universal calendar for achieving it.

Elaborations explain the terms and suggest approaches; the full descriptions remain at [S1]. Do not reproduce stories or other works without permission. In particular, recognizing First Peoples storytelling does not authorize retelling or adapting a community's story.

Grade 1 Early French Immersion uses a [distinct language-arts curriculum](../french-immersion-language-arts/curriculum.md), not a translation of this record.
