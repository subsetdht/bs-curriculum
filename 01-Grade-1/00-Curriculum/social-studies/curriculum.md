# Social Studies - Grade 1

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](../README.md) > Social Studies

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial subject expectations under [S2], sections 1-2.

**Document language:** English, separately from the language of classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Explore how people, communities, and local environments relate, using inquiry, evidence, perspective-taking, and judgments about fairness.

**Prerequisites:** Familiar experiences of families, groups, and places are useful starting points, not formal requirements. Do not assume all learners share the same family structure or community experiences.

**Materials:** None are required for the reference. Later activities should identify their own sources, materials, and permissions.

**How to use:** Read with [S1] and follow each locator to the official expectations. Local IDs are stable repository references for later resource alignment, not official curriculum codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

The relationship between a community and its surroundings works in both directions: people influence places, and places influence people's lives.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, the reciprocal relationship with the local environment.

### BI-02

Communities benefit when people understand rights and responsibilities, respect differences, and care for the local environment.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, rights/roles/responsibilities and healthy, diverse communities.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Ask questions, gather and interpret information, and communicate findings or decisions. Examine different sources for clues and use them to make supported inferences.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, inquiry processes and evidence.

### CC-02

Explain why familiar people, places, objects, or events matter. Put things in sequence, consider what changes or continues, and recognize causes and consequences in everyday life.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, significance, continuity and change, and cause and consequence.

### CC-03

Explore how different people may see a familiar situation, place, issue, or event.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, perspective.

### CC-04

Notice fairness and unfairness in actions or decisions and consider suitable ways to respond.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, ethical judgment.

## Content

### C-01

Learn how the local community is organized, how it meets needs, and how people's roles, rights, and responsibilities contribute.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, community organization/needs and local roles/rights/responsibilities.

### C-02

Explore diverse cultures, backgrounds, and perspectives, alongside important events and developments in the local community and local First Peoples communities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, community diversity and key local events and developments.

### C-03

Recognize natural and human-made features of nearby places and explore the connections between a community and its environment.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, community/environment relationships and local environmental features.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The source's elaborations suggest examples and questions; the full guidance remains at [S1]. This reference does not prescribe one community history, celebration, field trip, or textbook for every BC learner.

Local First Peoples histories and perspectives should be represented through appropriate sources and relationships. Do not invent a single account for all communities. Use inclusive activities that do not require learners to disclose private family information or personal experiences.
