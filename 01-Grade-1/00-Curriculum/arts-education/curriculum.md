# Arts Education - Grade 1

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 1](../README.md) > Arts Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language and Early French Immersion; shared provincial expectations.

**Document language:** English, separately from the language of classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Explore and communicate through dance, drama, music, and visual arts, developing creative processes, skills, reflection, and connections with community.

**Prerequisites:** Play, movement, listening, and making experiences offer useful entry points. No prior artistic training is assumed by this reference.

**Materials:** None are required for reading. Activities should specify suitable tools, materials, permissions, and accessible ways to participate.

**How to use:** Read with [S1] and follow the source locators. Local IDs make resource alignment traceable without implying official ministry codes or a prescribed sequence of projects.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Art can communicate personal and community identity.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, expression of individuals and community.

### BI-02

Purposeful artistic play creates opportunities to explore questions and possibilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, inquiry through purposeful play.

### BI-03

The four art forms offer different ways to make meaning, share ideas, and connect with other people.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, dance/drama/music/visual arts and connections through the arts.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Experiment with artistic elements, materials, movement, processes, and tools. Create individually and with others, drawing on imagination, questions, play, and experiences of self and community.

**Source locator:** [S1], Exploring and creating, exploration, collaborative/individual creation, and expressions of self/community.

### CC-02

Notice how artists work, develop technical and creative processes across art forms, and reflect on links between making art and other experiences.

**Source locator:** [S1], Reasoning and reflecting, artists' practices, skill development, and reflection.

### CC-03

Interpret symbols and use the arts to communicate feelings, stories, observations, and ideas. Describe and respond to artworks, document and share creative work in different ways, and apply learning with increasing sophistication.

**Source locator:** [S1], Communicating and documenting, symbols, expression, responses, documentation/sharing, and developing application of content.

## Content

### C-01

Explore dance through body, space, energy, timing, relationships, and form. In drama, consider characters and how time, place, and plot shape an experience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, elements of dance and drama.

### C-02

Explore musical beat, rhythm, tempo, pitch, dynamics, and form. In visual art, work with line, shape, texture, and colour, including pattern and repetition.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, music elements and visual-art design elements/principles.

### C-03

Learn about artistic processes, materials, movement, tools, techniques, and technologies. Explore notation for sounds, ideas, and movement, different dramatic forms, and symbols that carry meaning.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, arts processes/tools, notation, dramatic forms, and symbolism.

### C-04

Encounter traditional and contemporary Indigenous arts and ways of making, as well as local art and traditions from diverse cultures and communities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, traditional/contemporary Aboriginal arts and diverse local works and traditions.

### C-05

Understand personal and shared responsibilities for making, experiencing, and sharing art in a safe setting.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, responsibility in a safe learning environment.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Arts Education includes all four art forms; it is not only drawing or craft. The source describes its listed elements as non-exhaustive. Its elaborations clarify terms and examples and remain at [S1], rather than becoming a required project list here.

Exposure to Indigenous arts does not grant permission to copy designs, performances, songs, or cultural practices. Use appropriate guidance and permissions. Avoid requiring public performance or sharing of a learner's personal story as the only way to demonstrate learning.
