## Career Life Education

**Content status:** Source-checked summary available.

[Course landing](10-Grade-10/00-Curriculum/career-education/career-life-education/README.md) | [Curriculum summary](10-Grade-10/00-Curriculum/career-education/career-life-education/curriculum.md) | [Big Ideas](10-Grade-10/00-Curriculum/career-education/career-life-education/curriculum.md#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](10-Grade-10/00-Curriculum/career-education/career-life-education/curriculum.md#curricular-competencies) | [Content](10-Grade-10/00-Curriculum/career-education/career-life-education/curriculum.md#content)

**Official BC entry:** [Career Life Education](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/career-education/all/career-life-education), English learning standards.

**Scope and requirement:** The regular Dogwood requires four CLE and four CLC credits. Neither course has a designated teaching grade: the [handbook][handbook], printed p.41, permits starting in **10, 11, or 12**. The [CLE registry][cle-registry] assigns administrative Grade 10 credit, not a mandated delivery year.

| Reporting language | Four-credit option | Two-part option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | `CLE` | `CLEA` and `CLEB`, two credits each |
| French | `CLEF` | `CLEAF` and `CLEBF`, two credits each |

These are reporting options for one curriculum, not three standards sets. The [registry][registry] identifies instruction language separately.

**Placement:** The [Grade 10 course landing](10-Grade-10/00-Curriculum/career-education/career-life-education/README.md) is the approved **administrative/credit-level host**, supported by registry entry **3201860**, TRAX `CLE` / MyEd BC `MCLE-10`. Its record retains `source.grade_scope: ["10", "11", "12"]` and explains that the target directory is not a prescribed delivery year. Keep this entry and the shared record linked from all three senior overviews as their content is integrated. `CLE` and `all` are not grades.

**Programs:** Shared expectations for the four programs identified [below](#programs-and-languages); French reporting options are not a separate immersion or Francophone curriculum.

## Career Life Connections

**Content status:** Course inventory and source scope reviewed; needs summary and integration.

**Official BC entry:** [Career Life Connections course list](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/career-education/clc/courses), with one [CLC curriculum](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/career-education/all/career-life-connections), in English. The list incorrectly labels that row "Career Life Education"; its destination and document filenames identify CLC. The guide and FAQ are supporting documents, not additional courses.

| Reporting language | Four-credit option | Two-part option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | `CLC` | `CLCA` and `CLCB`, two credits each |
| French | `CLCF` | `CLCAF` and `CLCBF`, two credits each |

**Scope and requirement:** CLC's four credits count toward the Dogwood's Grade 12-level minimum; CLE does not. CLC includes school-approved career-life exploration of **30 hours or more** and a capstone. Exploration can include purposeful projects, not just employment or volunteering. The capstone is not a separate course. See the [policy guide][policy-guide], pp.15, 20-21, and [Career Education guide][career-guide], pp.7-14.

**Placement:** `12-Grade-12/00-Curriculum/career-education/career-life-connections/` is the approved **administrative/credit-level host**. [Registry entry 3201862][clc-registry], TRAX `CLC` / MyEd BC `MCLC-12`, establishes the credit level, not a teaching year. Retain `source.grade_scope: ["10", "11", "12"]`, this anchor, and links from all 10-12 overviews. Programs are shared as described [below](#programs-and-languages).

**Credit boundaries:** Relevant WEX/WRK experience may contribute to CLC exploration with mentor guidance and school approval, but CLC exploration hours cannot count toward WEX/WRK. Workforce Training Certificate 12 does not satisfy CLC exploration. None replaces the whole course or capstone. See the [FAQ][career-faq], questions 4-5, and [exploration policy][clc-policy].

**Adult-program boundary:** The [handbook][handbook], p.41, restricts `CLE 12` and `CLCG 12` to the Adult Graduation Program; the registry lists French counterparts `CLEF 12` and `CLCGF12`. These adult elective options are not extra ordinary Grade 12 requirements.

## Additional Offerings

**Content status:** All four direct curricula inventoried; grade placements established. Needs grade-owner summaries and navigation. Related pathways have separate limits below.

**Official BC entry:** [Additional Offerings](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/additional-offerings), in English. The [French catalog](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/fr/curriculum/additional-offerings) has the same four entries. These represent **six credit-bearing courses**: WRK's A/B courses share each published grade-level curriculum.

| [Work Experience 12B](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/additional-offerings/12B/work-experience) | `WEX 12B`, four credits | 12 | `12-Grade-12/00-Curriculum/career-education/work-experience-12b/` |

| [Youth Work in Trades 12](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/additional-offerings/12/youth-work-in-trades) | `WRK 12A` and `WRK 12B`, four credits each | 12 | `12-Grade-12/00-Curriculum/career-education/youth-work-in-trades/` |

The [registry][registry] also lists `WEXF 12A`, `WEXF 12B`, and `WRKF 11A`, `WRKF 11B`, `WRKF 12A`, `WRKF 12B` as four-credit French counterparts. These language/reporting variants do not require duplicate standards records.

**WEX boundaries:** Optional Grade 12 courses, not a compulsory final-year schedule. Keep A/B distinct: B develops prior work-experience learning. School arrangements, documented learning, placement-specific safety orientation, monitoring, and teacher evaluation are required; an ordinary job does not automatically earn credit. Paid placements have specific [policy conditions][wex-policy]. Age 14 is a condition of provincial coverage for qualifying unpaid placements, not a curriculum grade. See the [WEX guide][wex-guide], pp.1-2, 5-9, 12-15.

**WRK boundaries:** The [guide][wrk-guide], pp.1, 3-4, 6-7, 10, establishes up to 16 credits and 480 training hours. Participants are school-aged, at least 14, and in 10-12, independently of the published curriculum grade. A recognized trade, SkilledTradesBC registration/sponsor, training plans, and paid apprenticeship work are required; prior-work recognition is conditional. Use one summary per grade-level standards set, listing both A/B courses. Current procedures use SkilledTradesBC; curriculum pages retain older Industry Training Authority terminology.

**Curriculum mapping:** Place these records under `00-Curriculum`, with ADST cross-navigation, not duplicate records. Four WEX or WRK credits may meet the ADST component of the Arts Education/ADST requirement ([policy guide][policy-guide], pp.22, 31). This does not make them compulsory. `03-Additional-Content` means unmapped enrichment, not BC Additional Offerings.

### Source conflicts and limits

The catalog labels the WEX guide "2023", but the document is dated **August 2024**. WRK is linked as "2025"; no separate cover revision date was established. Catalog labels are not curriculum editions.

The November 2023 STX table lists four-credit A and two/four-credit B options. The registry allows two/four for both (for example, [STX 12A][stx-registry]); use it for credit allowances, and the guide for separate funding conditions.

**Unresolved ADST conflict:** The 2026-27 guide, p.31, says Sampler and Train may meet ADST. The [August 2026 dual-credit policy][dual-credit] limits dual credit to elective/Grade 12 credits, excluding other graduation requirements. Obtain Ministry confirmation before promising ADST substitution for dual credit. WEX, WRK, and STX have separate supporting evidence.

Current BAA policy prohibits modified/remedial courses and significant overlap with Ministry outcomes; do not follow the older WEX guide's suggestion of remedial BAA WEX.

Preserve STX's module outcomes, Sampler's competency lines, and trade-specific outlines. Do not manufacture Big Ideas, Curricular Competencies, or Content to fill the template; leave record-format decisions with the coordinator.

Native HTML and local PDF rendering/OCR recovered the direct standards and cited guide sections. WTC's full guide/certificate inventory remains unresolved. Provider-specific outlines and local BAA/IDS frameworks remain prerequisites for future conditional records.

## Programs and languages

**Shared expectations:** CLE/CLC and direct WEX/WRK references support `english-language`, `early-french-immersion`, `late-french-immersion`, and `francophone`, subject to participation requirements. This does not establish local course availability.

The [Immersion policy][immersion-policy] establishes Early/Late Immersion separately and parallel curriculum. The [2026-27 policy guide][policy-guide], Appendix 4, pp.40-41, explicitly says both Immersion and Francophone programs parallel the regular English program in structure/content; Francophone instruction is French except English Language Arts. This provincial statement, not translated PDFs alone, supports shared non-language expectations. Pages 15-16 retain Dogwood requirements within distinct dual-diploma pathways.

This page and the substantive curriculum/policy sources reviewed are **English**; the French catalog is **French navigation**. French reporting alternatives were read in the English registry, not verified as translated curriculum editions. No French repository edition or classroom resource is supplied. English sources do not prescribe English instruction or make English workplaces/certificates suitable for every program.

For STX's shared foundation, identify the local modules. For Sampler, Train, WTC, and local offerings, verify provider eligibility and instruction language separately; no blanket all-program delivery claim is made.
