## Program coverage

**Program coverage:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone share these provincial non-language course expectations. The programs remain distinct; this coverage does not assert that every school offers all 14 courses.

- The current [2026/27 Graduation Policy Guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/graduation/graduation-policy-guide.pdf), **printed pp. 40-41**, explicitly requires both French Immersion and Francophone programs to parallel the regular curriculum. This is the direct shared-content authority, used with each actual course source.
- The [provincial French Immersion policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/french-immersion-program) also requires parallel regular-program content and applies its secondary guidance to Late Immersion in 8-12.
- The [provincial comparison of French education programs](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/learn-in-french/kindergarten-to-grade-12/kindergarten-to-grade-12-compare-french-programs) identifies Francophone education as a distinct K-12 program whose subjects, except English Language Arts, are taught in French.
- The guide's **printed pp. 15-17**, and the [provincial graduation requirements](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/graduation), distinguish Dogwood and Dual Dogwood requirements, including program-specific language and French-delivered credits. The common requirements include Arts Education and/or ADST course credit; they do not make each individual ADST course compulsory.

Program-source review was completed **2026-09-14**, with the guide's relevant sections verified in the coordinated provincial-policy review. Applicability rests on the guide's explicit parallel-curriculum requirement and actual ADST standards, **not on a translation or the comparison chart alone**. The chart supplies program and language context, not proof of identical content, mandatory courses, or local offerings.

**Document language:** All 14 summaries are English (`en-CA`), based on complete English (`en`) course pages and their elaborations. French source editions are linked in the provincial catalog, but no French-language repository editions are supplied. Summary/source language does not determine classroom language or local course availability.

## Course boundaries

- **Technology Explorations 10** has its own published standards and requires at least **six listed content entries from at least two curricular areas**. Its source labels choices from Woodwork, Metalwork, Electronics and Robotics, Power Technology, and Drafting. These are contributing areas, not five additional courses to count, five complete curricula to import, or aliases.
- **Electronics and Robotics 10** is one combined published course, not two separately published curriculum choices.
- **Culinary Arts 10** and **Food Studies 10** have separate standards: food-service and teaching-kitchen practice in the former; meal design, eating practices, and food systems in the latter.
- No additional grouped course aliases are named by this course list. English and French source links and multiple download formats refer to language/format editions, not extra courses.
- These are original core summaries, not full transcriptions, equipment instructions, activities, assessments, or source-document mirrors. The complete official expectations and elaborations remain authoritative.
