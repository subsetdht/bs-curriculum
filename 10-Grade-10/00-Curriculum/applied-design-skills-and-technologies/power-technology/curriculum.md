# Power Technology - Grade 10

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 10 overview](../../README.md) > [ADST courses](../README.md) > [Power Technology](README.md)

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial subject content supported by [S2] and the [reviewed provincial program evidence](../README.md#program-coverage). Francophone delivery is in French; local course availability is not implied.

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`), based on the English source; this does not prescribe classroom language.

**Learning objective:** Understand mechanical and energy systems and their maintenance, connecting safe, sequenced work with responsible choices about power, transportation, and environmental impacts.

**Prerequisites:** Familiarity with measurement, mechanisms, and earlier design processes may help; no formal course-entry requirement is established here.

**Materials:** The reference and access to [S1]; no engine, repair tools, fuel, or powered equipment is needed for reading.

**How to use:** Compare these groups with official expectations and elaborations using the locators. Local IDs are repository references, not ministry codes. This is a named Grade 10 course choice rather than a requirement for every learner.

## Content

### C-01

Understand internal and external combustion, combustion-engine components, engine terminology, and non-fuel power systems. Connect terminology with operating principles and system classification.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, combustion, engine components, non-fuel power systems, and engine terminology.

### C-02

Understand disassembly and assembly sequences and the tools used in mechanical repair and maintenance. Relate fastening and fitting choices, operation-specific torque and tolerance, lubrication, and antifriction features to reliable mechanical work.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, disassembly/assembly, repair tools, torques/tolerances, fasteners/fittings, and lubrication/antifriction.

### C-03

Understand energy transfer and conversion, including hydraulic and pneumatic systems and mechanical transmission systems such as gears, pulleys, and chains.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, hydraulic/pneumatic systems, transfer/conversion of energy, and energy transmission/conversion systems.

### C-04

Examine technologies that reduce energy use and waste and recognize alternate energy sources. Consider the historical and possible future effects of energy, power, and transportation systems on society and the environment.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, energy/waste reduction technologies, historical/future system impacts, and alternate energy sources.

## Elaborations and boundaries

This is not exclusively an automotive or combustion-engine course: non-fuel power, alternate energy, and broader system impacts are explicit content. The source's examples clarify categories without prescribing one engine, repair task, or energy technology.

This reference does not supply operating procedures, safe torque values, or authorization to perform repairs. Practical work needs suitable supervision and current equipment-specific safety arrangements. First Peoples and other cultural knowledge in research and feedback requires appropriate protocols and permissions.
