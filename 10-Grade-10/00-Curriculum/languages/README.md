# Languages - Grade 10

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [10](../README.md) > Languages

**Content status:** Summary available for all **9 of 9** published course entries in the linked 10 catalog. Course inventory complete; 9 named course landings and 9 source-checked `core-summary` records. These condensed references are not the full official curriculum or a complete all-program collection.

**Official BC entry:** [Languages 10](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/languages/10/courses)

**Program coverage:** English-language: all 9 records. Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone: the 8 additional-language choices other than Core French, where offered. The [2026-27 Graduation Policy Guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/graduation/graduation-policy-guide.pdf), printed pp. **40-41**, establishes parallel regular-curriculum content for immersion and Francophone programs; the [provincial immersion policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/french-immersion-program) supplies common secondary guidance for both immersion entry pathways. Combined with each actual course source, these support shared additional-language content, not local availability or a prescribed classroom support language. These courses do not replace program language arts. Core French remains mapped to English-language only; neither the parallel-content policy nor a translated document makes it French Immersion Language Arts or Francophone Language Arts.

**Document and source languages:** All 9 summaries are English (`en-CA`), and all 9 reviewed primary course sources are English (`en`). French editions are linked from the official catalog but have not been separately reviewed here; no local French-language editions are provided. Document/source language is distinct from the language being learned, shown below.
