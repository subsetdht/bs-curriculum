# Arts Education - Grade 10

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [10](../README.md) > Arts Education

**Content status:** Summary available for all **16 independently linked curricula** in the reviewed Grade 10 course inventory.

**Official BC entry:** [Arts Education 10](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/arts-education/10/courses)

**Inventory coverage:** The official list contains 16 curriculum entries in five groups: 2 cross-disciplinary/interdisciplinary, 4 Dance, 3 Drama, 3 Music, and 4 Visual Arts. Choral Music and Instrumental Music additionally name **7 included options**. This directory has **16 source-checked curriculum records, 16 corresponding course/family landings, and 7 alias landings**. The aliases are not seven additional independent curricula; English/French download entries are not additional courses.

**Program coverage:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone for all 16 records. The [provincial French Immersion policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/french-immersion-program) requires parallel curriculum content and applies the secondary guidance to Late Immersion in Grades 8-12.

**Shared-program evidence:** The [2026-27 B.C. Graduation Program Policy Guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/graduation/graduation-policy-guide.pdf), Appendix 4, printed **pp. 40-41**, introduces French Immersion on p. 40 and explicitly states on **p. 41** that both French Immersion and Francophone programs parallel the regular English program in structure and content. Together with the reviewed Arts course standards, this supports shared program applicability. Its Graduation Requirements section, printed **pp. 15-17**, distinguishes pathways, with the shared Dogwood and Dual Dogwood requirements on pp. 15-16. This evidence does not establish local course offerings.

**Program distinctions:** The [provincial comparison chart](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/learn-in-french/kindergarten-to-grade-12/kindergarten-to-grade-12-compare-french-programs) identifies program scope and language delivery, including Francophone K-12 instruction in French except English Language Arts. It is supporting context, not proof of shared curriculum content. Shared Arts mapping rests on the policy guide and actual course standards, not translations. Program evidence reviewed **2026-09-14**.

**Languages:** All written summaries are English (`en-CA`) and use reviewed English primary sources (`en`). French-language summary editions and teaching materials are not provided. Neither source nor summary language determines classroom instruction or which courses a school offers.

These are course choices, not a requirement for every learner to take every Arts course or to take Arts specifically in Grade 10. The [provincial graduation overview](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/graduation), checked 2026-09-14, describes a combined **4-credit Arts Education and/or ADST requirement across Grades 10, 11, or 12**. Consult graduation guidance and local course information separately; this inventory is not a graduation plan.
