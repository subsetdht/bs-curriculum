# Media Arts - Grade 10

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 10](../../README.md) > [Arts Education](../README.md) > Media Arts

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone. [S4], printed pp. 40-41, explicitly states that French Immersion and Francophone programs parallel the regular English program in structure and content; together with [S1], this supports shared Arts expectations. [S2] supplies Early/Late Immersion guidance. This does not establish local course availability.

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`); the reviewed source is English. Neither establishes classroom language or local course availability.

**Learning objective:** Develop an artistic voice through media production, thoughtful design decisions, interpretation, and responsible participation in digital culture.

**Prerequisites:** Experience interpreting images and experimenting with creative technologies may help; this reference sets no formal entry requirement.

**Materials:** No special materials are needed to read this reference.

**How to use:** Compare this Grade 10 course summary with [S1] and its elaborations. Item IDs are stable repository references, not official BC codes or a teaching sequence. This is one course choice, not a requirement to take every Arts course.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Purposeful artistic choices turn media technologies into expressive forms through the interaction of bodily experience and thought.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, artistic intention and the interplay of mind and body.

### BI-02

Media art can carry cultural stories and worldviews while helping people investigate identity and belonging.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, sharing traditions/perspectives/stories and exploring identity.

### BI-03

Artistic growth develops over time through patient work and reconsideration of creative decisions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, time, patience, and reflection.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Use sensory experience, imagination, inquiry, and varied inspirations to develop media works for an audience. Experiment with unfamiliar possibilities, communicate personal stories and values, and improve technical and problem-solving skills while using materials, equipment, and space responsibly.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Explore and create: inspiration, creative risks, audience, personal voice, skill development, and safe use.

### CC-02

Resolve creative problems by examining how artists use technology, materials, processes, and settings. Evaluate choices throughout planning, making, and interpretation; develop reasoned personal responses to aesthetic questions and consider contextual influences on art.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Reason and reflect: challenges, artistic methods, creative choices, aesthetic questions, and contexts.

### CC-03

Record and share creative development, appreciate others' media works, and communicate ideas and feelings. Respond to social and environmental concerns through media art with respect for people and place.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Communicate and document: documentation/sharing, expression, respect, and social/environmental issues.

### CC-04

Investigate First Peoples perspectives, local cultural knowledge, and other ways of knowing through media art. Practise digital citizenship across the creative process, including respect for privacy and cultural values.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Connect and expand: First Peoples knowledge and digital citizenship.

## Content

### C-01

Understand how visual elements and design principles organize media experiences, including time, depth, scale, sequencing, and synchronization. Learn ways to develop images and use symbols and metaphors to convey perspectives.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, elements/principles of design, image development strategies, and symbols/metaphors.

### C-02

Develop a working understanding of media technologies, materials, techniques, image sources, genres, and specialist vocabulary. Technology choices include established and emerging forms; standards-compliant work involves relevant layout, markup, web, or other digital-media conventions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, media technologies, standards-compliant technology, image sources/genres, vocabulary, and materials/processes/techniques.

### C-03

Distinguish planning a production, creating its media, and refining it into a coherent finished work. These pre-production, production, and post-production stages involve different decisions and skills.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, media production skills and elaborations for the three production stages.

### C-04

Study media artists working across local, national, international, and intercultural contexts, including First Peoples stories, histories, and traditional and contemporary worldviews. Understand responsibilities concerning copyright, duplication, and appropriation of images, sound, and video.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, First Peoples/cross-cultural perspectives, media artists, and ethical/moral/legal considerations.

## Elaborations and boundaries

This is an Arts curriculum, not simply software training. The source's examples of animation, film, sound, websites, and emerging media illustrate possibilities, not a requirement to use every format or a particular product. Environmental responsibility includes considering material reuse and disposal.

Creative risk is informed experimentation, not disregard for safety, privacy, or rights. Cultural knowledge and online availability do not provide permission to reuse a community's stories, imagery, music, or recordings. Seek appropriate permissions and context; do not reproduce protected cultural works.
