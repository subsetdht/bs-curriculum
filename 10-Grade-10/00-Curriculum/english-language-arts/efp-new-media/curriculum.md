# English First Peoples New Media - Grade 10

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 10](../../README.md) > [English Language Arts](../README.md) > EFP New Media

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; an English Language Arts pathway distinct from French language arts. See [program and selection evidence](../README.md#programs-and-course-selection).

**Document language:** English; official source language: English.

**Learning objective:** Examine and create new-media communication with attention to First Peoples voices, cultural representation, and ethical digital participation.

**Prerequisites:** Prior experience interpreting varied texts and using digital media can help; this is not an official entry requirement.

**Materials:** This reference and official sources; no particular software, account, or recording device is required to use the reference.

**How to use:** Follow locators to [S1] rather than treating the local IDs as official codes. [S2] identifies a **2-credit** catalog component; graduation-course combinations are distinguished in the parent inventory.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Texts connect personal understanding with the wider world while carrying the influences of the communities, places, and times in which they were made.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, text/story and identity; constructed texts.

### BI-02

Authentic First Peoples self-representation can challenge injustice by allowing people to communicate their own experiences and perspectives.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, authentic First Peoples text, self-representation, and justice.

### BI-03

Digital participation extends across borders and involves rights as well as obligations to others.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, digital citizenship in a globalized society.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

**Comprehend and connect:** Recognize diverse First Peoples perspectives in stories, oral traditions, and new media. Explore authenticity and the effect of personal and cultural contexts on representation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, story/oral tradition, diversity, contexts/perspectives, and authenticity.

### CC-02

**Comprehend and connect:** Use comprehension strategies across media and investigate information from varied sources, judging its relevance, accuracy, and reliability. Think critically, creatively, and reflectively across texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, strategies, information evaluation, and thinking within/between/beyond texts.

### CC-03

**Comprehend and connect:** Investigate digital rights and responsibilities. Examine how media techniques and text structures shape meaning, and whether a medium suits its audience and purpose.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, digital citizenship, forms/features, new-media techniques/devices, and media suitability.

### CC-04

**Create and communicate:** Exchange viewpoints respectfully, respond to texts in varied ways, and communicate through purposeful speech, listening, and digital collaboration. Support opinions with evidence.

**Source locator:** [S1], Create and communicate, viewpoints, responses, speaking/listening, digital collaboration, and evidence.

### CC-05

**Create and communicate:** Select appropriate media and use writing and multimedia-design processes to develop and refine texts. Apply context-appropriate First Peoples and other Canadian conventions, intellectual-property rights, and community protocols.

**Source locator:** [S1], Create and communicate, media selection, writing/multimedia design, refinement, conventions, and rights/protocols.

## Content

### C-01

Know how genres, narrative organization, purpose, and interactivity work in new media and other texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Text forms and genres; Text features and structures, including interactivity.

### C-02

Understand First Peoples themes involving land, spirituality, family, Elders, community, identity, colonial disruption, cultural continuity, healing, and reconciliation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Common themes in First Peoples texts and Reconciliation in Canada.

### C-03

Understand the purposes of oral traditions and protocols governing ownership and use of First Peoples oral knowledge.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, First Peoples oral traditions; Protocols.

### C-04

Know reading, speaking, metacognitive, and writing strategies alongside processes for multimedia presentations.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Strategies and processes.

### C-05

Study stylistic, literary, and media devices together with usage, conventions, citation, and acknowledgement.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions, including media elements/devices.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The focus is media literacy and communication grounded in First Peoples perspectives, not training for a particular platform. The examples in [S2] are possible focus areas rather than required productions.

First Peoples communities are diverse; authenticity requires more than an Indigenous topic. Online access does not grant permission to remix, publish, or translate community knowledge. This record includes neither cultural works nor digital activities. It is distinct from [New Media](../new-media/curriculum.md), even where language-arts processes overlap.
