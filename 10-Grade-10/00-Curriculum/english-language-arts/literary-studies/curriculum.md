# Literary Studies - Grade 10

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 10](../../README.md) > [English Language Arts](../README.md) > Literary Studies

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; an English Language Arts choice distinct from French language arts. See [program and pathway evidence](../README.md#programs-and-course-selection).

**Document language:** English; official source language: English.

**Learning objective:** Broaden literary understanding through close, reflective engagement with varied texts, informed interpretations, and communication about literature.

**Prerequisites:** Experience sustaining reading and discussing interpretations is helpful, not an official course-entry rule.

**Materials:** This reference and its sources; it prescribes no purchased anthology or required novel.

**How to use:** Follow each locator to [S1] for official wording and elaborations. Repository IDs are not ministry codes. [S2] identifies a **2-credit** option; do not confuse it with the separate EFP Literary Studies choice.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Literature can enlarge understanding of identity and the world, while readers' different perspectives lead to different interpretations.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, exploration of text/story and different worldviews.

### BI-02

Texts arise in social, cultural, geographical, and historical circumstances, and their language can influence the thinking of others.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, constructed texts and language shaping ideas.

### BI-03

Questioning the messages encountered through reading, listening, and viewing supports informed participation in society.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, questioning texts and educated/engaged citizenship.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

**Comprehend and connect:** Read for enjoyment and personal aims. Use purposeful comprehension and information-gathering strategies, examine source reliability, and study how textual form and organization influence meaning.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, enjoyment/goals, information sources, relevance/accuracy/reliability, strategies, and text forms/features.

### CC-02

**Comprehend and connect:** Connect interpretations across texts and with lived experience. Examine the values, contexts, and identities they convey, while detecting bias, contradictions, and distortions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, thinking across texts, contexts/perspectives, identity, personal connections, and bias.

### CC-03

**Comprehend and connect:** Appreciate diverse First Peoples perspectives and oral traditions. Consider how land and place influence First Peoples and other Canadian texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, First Peoples story, diversity, and influence of land/place.

### CC-04

**Create and communicate:** Develop understanding through respectful exchanges and varied responses to texts. Use speaking and listening appropriately in different settings and justify opinions with evidence.

**Source locator:** [S1], Create and communicate, viewpoints, responses, speaking/listening, and evidence.

### CC-05

**Create and communicate:** Plan and improve engaging texts through writing and design processes. Make purposeful language choices, use Canadian written conventions appropriately, and recognize intellectual property through acknowledgement and citation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Create and communicate, writing/design, refining texts, conventions, and acknowledgements/citations.

## Content

### C-01

Know genres, structural patterns, and presentation features that support literary interpretation, including narrative structures found in First Peoples texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Text forms and genres; Text features and structures.

### C-02

Understand protocols governing ownership and sharing of First Peoples oral texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, protocols related to ownership of First Peoples oral texts.

### C-03

Know approaches to reading, oral communication, metacognition, writing, and design that support both understanding and creating texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Strategies and processes.

### C-04

Study how voice, style, literary devices, usage, and conventions work. Distinguish directly stated meaning from inferences and know how to cite sources.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions: style/voice, literary devices, literal/inferential meaning, and citation.

## Elaborations and boundaries

No single literary canon, author, historical period, or book list is imposed by this summary. [S2]'s possible areas of focus offer choices. Literary study includes oral, visual, and digital texts as well as print.

This is not the [EFP Literary Studies record](../efp-literary-studies/curriculum.md), whose expectations centre First Peoples texts. Both retain First Peoples perspectives, but their scope and competencies should not be merged. Community permissions remain necessary for culturally held stories and knowledge.
