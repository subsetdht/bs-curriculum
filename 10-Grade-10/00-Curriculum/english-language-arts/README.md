## Programs and course selection

- The [2026-27 B.C. Graduation Program Policy Guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/graduation/graduation-policy-guide.pdf), printed **pp. 15-16**, establishes Dogwood and dual-diploma language-arts requirements. Printed **pp. 40-41**, Appendix 4, explains immersion programming and explicitly states that both French Immersion and Francophone programs parallel the regular English program in structure and content. Read with the actual ELA course standards and the pathway evidence below, this supports the four program labels on these records. It does not establish local course availability, French-language resources, or interchangeability of English, first-language French, and immersion language arts.
- The [provincial ELA introduction](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/curriculum/english-language-arts/introduction), "Features of the English Language Arts curriculum," identifies English and EFP in Grades 10-12 as academically equivalent routes satisfying ELA requirements. EFP is not restricted to Indigenous learners.
- [Provincial graduation guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/graduation) requires Grade 10 language-arts credit, not completion of every choice in this inventory. The official DOCX introduction linked from each record labels **each of these nine choices 2 credits**. Two 2-credit choices provide the 4-credit Grade 10 total; individual course selection and reporting should be checked against current provincial requirements and actual school offerings.
- In particular, **none of the four EFP catalog choices is a separate 4-credit course**. The province's [Indigenous-focused recognized course list](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/indigenous-focused-graduation-requirements), under "Recognized Course List," explicitly marks all four as **2-credit courses**, each meeting half the 4-credit Indigenous-focused requirement. Do not confuse these with the named combined EFP courses at Grade 11, or count one EFP 10 choice as satisfying a 4-credit requirement by itself.
- The [French Immersion policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/french-immersion-program) requires ELA from Grade 4 onward in Early Immersion and directs Late Immersion Grades 8-12 to the same secondary guidance. Both apply at Grade 10.
- The [graduation page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/graduation), "Francophone Dual Dogwood," explicitly requires English Language Arts at Grade 10 as well as first-language French. Francophone applicability here concerns that English component, not French-language instructional suitability.

For the other language-arts components, use [Francophone Language Arts](../francophone-language-arts/README.md) or [French Immersion Language Arts](../french-immersion-language-arts/README.md). School timetables, course packaging, and local text choices are implementation matters, not additional provincial learning standards.
