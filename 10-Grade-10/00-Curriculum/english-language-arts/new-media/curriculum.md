# New Media - Grade 10

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 10](../../README.md) > [English Language Arts](../README.md) > New Media

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, Late French Immersion, and Francophone; this English Language Arts option does not replace French language arts. See [program evidence](../README.md#programs-and-course-selection).

**Document language:** English; official source language: English.

**Learning objective:** Interpret and create communication in changing media environments, connecting critical literacy with thoughtful digital participation.

**Prerequisites:** Familiarity with varied texts and basic digital communication is helpful, not a formal entry requirement.

**Materials:** This reference and official sources. No particular device, software platform, or online account is prescribed.

**How to use:** Compare the grouped expectations with [S1]; local IDs are repository identifiers. [S2] labels this a **2-credit** choice, separate from EFP New Media.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Stories and other communications help people explore identity and experience, but audiences interpret them through differing perspectives.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, exploration of text/story and different worldviews.

### BI-02

Language influences people, and texts reflect the social, cultural, historical, and geographical circumstances of their creation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, language shaping ideas and constructed texts.

### BI-03

Participating in digitally connected societies brings both rights and responsibilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, digital citizens in a globalized society.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

**Comprehend and connect:** Consider the complexities of digital citizenship. Read for enjoyment and personal goals, gather information, test its reliability, and use strategies suited to varied and multimodal texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, digital citizenship, reading goals, information sources, reliability, and strategies.

### CC-02

**Comprehend and connect:** Investigate how presentation and structure affect meaning. Think across texts, connect them with experience and identity, and detect bias, inconsistency, and distortion.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, forms/features, critical/creative/reflective thinking, personal/social identities, and bias.

### CC-03

**Comprehend and connect:** Explore how stories and oral traditions express First Peoples perspectives, and how new media and other texts represent diverse First Peoples cultures.

**Source locator:** [S1], Comprehend and connect, First Peoples oral tradition and cultural diversity in new media.

### CC-04

**Create and communicate:** Exchange viewpoints respectfully and respond to texts in personal, imaginative, and critical ways. Use spoken interaction and digital collaboration to communicate with audiences within and beyond the classroom; support opinions with evidence.

**Source locator:** [S1], Create and communicate, viewpoints, responses, speaking/listening, digital collaboration, and evidence.

### CC-05

**Create and communicate:** Plan and revise original texts using writing and design processes. Improve their clarity and effect while applying suitable Canadian conventions and crediting others' intellectual property.

**Source locator:** [S1], Create and communicate, writing/design, refining, transforming ideas, conventions, and citations.

## Content

### C-01

Know how genres, multimodal features, and interactivity shape communication, alongside narrative structures represented in First Peoples texts.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Text forms and genres; Text features and structures: interactivity, multimodal texts, and narrative structures.

### C-02

Understand ownership protocols for First Peoples oral texts; digital availability does not establish permission to redistribute them.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, protocols related to ownership of First Peoples oral texts.

### C-03

Know reading, oral-language, and metacognitive strategies, together with writing, new-media design, and multimedia presentation processes.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Strategies and processes.

### C-04

Develop knowledge of stylistic effects, literary techniques, usage, conventions, and citation for effective media communication.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions.

## Elaborations and boundaries

This language-arts course is not interchangeable with a technical computing course. [S2]'s media examples are possible emphases, not required platforms or public posting tasks. Written, spoken, visual, and digital communication all remain in scope.

Keep this option distinct from [EFP New Media](../efp-new-media/curriculum.md). Digital citizenship and intellectual property matter in both; community-specific permission is necessary before using culturally held material, regardless of whether it is already online.
