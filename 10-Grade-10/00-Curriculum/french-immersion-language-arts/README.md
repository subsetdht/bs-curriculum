## Programs and course selection

The [French Immersion Program policy](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/legislation-policy/public-schools/french-immersion-program) explicitly distinguishes Early Immersion (K-12) and Late Immersion (6-12). Its Late Immersion secondary section directs Grades 8-12 to the same secondary immersion guidance. This evidence supports both pathways at Grade 10, not assumed equivalence of their earlier entry curricula.

The [provincial graduation page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/graduation), "French Immersion Dual Dogwood," lists Français langue seconde-immersion 10 for **4 credits**, in addition to meeting Dogwood requirements. It is not an alternative to the [English Language Arts component](../english-language-arts/README.md). Upper-year immersion choices must be checked in their own grade inventories, not copied into this one.

The [2026-27 B.C. Graduation Program Policy Guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/graduation/graduation-policy-guide.pdf), printed **pp. 15-16**, confirms the Dogwood and immersion dual-diploma requirements. Printed **pp. 40-41**, Appendix 4, describes immersion and explicitly states that it parallels the regular English program in structure and content. This program-level statement does not replace the distinct immersion language-arts standards with ELA, establish a local offering, or make an English summary a French classroom resource.
