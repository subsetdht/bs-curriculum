# Études littéraires et artistiques + production écrite - Grade 10

[Curriculum index](../../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 10](../../README.md) > [Francophone Language Arts](../README.md) > Literary and artistic studies + written production

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** Francophone only; first-language French curriculum, distinct from French Immersion, Core French, and English Language Arts.

**Document language:** English; official source and course title: French. This original synthesis is not an authorized translation or a French classroom resource. Locators use English glosses.

**Learning objective:** Connect interpretation of Francophone literature and art with purposeful, reflective written expression.

**Prerequisites:** Experience reading and composing French texts is helpful, not an official course-entry requirement.

**Materials:** The reference and linked official sources; no anthology or writing software is prescribed.

**How to use:** Read these groups alongside [S1], retaining its French terminology and elaborations as authoritative. Local IDs are repository references. [S2] describes a **4-credit combined course**, one of three options rather than an additional requirement for everyone.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Thinking and talking about texts can deepen understanding of oneself and others; precise language strengthens critical and creative thought.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas (English gloss), reflection/exchanges and linguistic precision.

### BI-02

Form shapes aesthetic experience and meaning. Literary and artistic works express situated perspectives on the cultures and histories of the Francophone world.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas (English gloss), textual form, Francophone culture/history, and time/space.

### BI-03

Creative production requires planning and the discipline to sustain and improve one's work.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas (English gloss), creative process, self-discipline, and planning.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

**Explore and reflect:** Connect themes and cultural references across texts, identify central questions, and consider diverse contexts. Relate one's own production to experience and the wider world.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies (English gloss), Explore and reflect: themes/cultural markers, textual problem, diversity/context, and self/world in production.

### CC-02

**Explore and reflect:** Appreciate the roles of Indigenous stories and oral and artistic traditions in communicating perspectives, values, and beliefs.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies (English gloss), Explore and reflect: Indigenous story, oral tradition, and artistic tradition.

### CC-03

**Explore and reflect:** Investigate symbolic meaning and the effects of language, structure, technique, and style on audiences. Evaluate the usefulness and validity of information before taking a position.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies (English gloss), Explore and reflect: symbolism, audience effects, and information relevance/validity.

### CC-04

**Create and communicate:** Develop organized ideas through writing and design processes. Summarize messages, consider multiple perspectives, and support arguments with suitable evidence and references.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies (English gloss), Create and communicate: processes, summary, perspectives, and arguments.

### CC-05

**Create and communicate:** Respond to and produce texts with personal, creative, and critical engagement. Use French conventions and stylistic techniques purposefully, while communicating ethically.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies (English gloss), Create and communicate: grammar/syntax/punctuation, stylistic effects, ethics, response, and production.

## Content

### C-01

Know how genre and textual structure organize meaning, and how references acknowledge source material.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), Textual organization.

### C-02

Understand rhetorical, stylistic, and analytical tools for interpreting literature, along with permission protocols for Indigenous stories.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), Literary tools.

### C-03

Know the writing process and techniques for shaping a position and message. Understand explicit and implied meaning, the communication model, memorization strategies, and source acknowledgement.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), Strategies, including writing process and techniques.

### C-04

Study connotation and denotation, registers, discourse types, syntax, and vocabulary as resources for written expression.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), Language tools.

### C-05

Understand how figurative language, context, and visual support enrich interpretation and communication.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content (English gloss), Enrichment tools.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The combined course retains literary and artistic study rather than becoming only composition practice. The source frames learning through varied genres and artistic movements; its variety elaboration refers to works from at least two different periods, places, and genres. Examples of movements and genres do not establish a required reading list.

Oral, written, visual, digital, and print texts remain relevant. Indigenous perspectives are integral to this subject, and source acknowledgement does not replace permission to reuse community knowledge. This record contains no literary works, writing activities, or assessment tasks.
