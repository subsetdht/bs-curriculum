## Programs and course selection

The [official Francophone introduction](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/fr/curriculum/francais-langue-premiere/introduction) identifies first-language French goals, Francophone identity, and the Grade 10-11 options. It also explains that oral, written, visual, digital, and multimodal communication remains relevant in each option.

The [provincial graduation requirements](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/graduation), "Francophone Dual Dogwood," require a Grade 10 Français langue première course worth 4 credits, alongside English Language Arts 10. Each course's official French DOCX description, linked from its record, confirms the **4-credit combined course** label. The requirement does not make all three alternatives compulsory.

The [2026-27 B.C. Graduation Program Policy Guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/graduation/graduation-policy-guide.pdf), printed **p. 16**, confirms these separate French and English requirements. Printed **p. 41**, Appendix 4, explicitly states that the Francophone program parallels the regular English program in structure and content, while describing its distinct first-language environment and French instruction except for ELA. This is provincial program evidence, not an inference from translated documents. It does not merge the language-arts curricula or establish local course availability.

The ministry's [French-program comparison](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/learn-in-french/kindergarten-to-grade-12/kindergarten-to-grade-12-compare-french-programs) distinguishes Francophone education from immersion and identifies French instruction in subject areas other than English Language Arts. An English summary does not change the language of the program. For the English component, use the separate [English Language Arts inventory](../english-language-arts/README.md).
