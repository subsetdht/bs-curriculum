The [2026-27 Graduation Program Policy Guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/graduation/graduation-policy-guide.pdf), printed pp. 15-17 and 21, distinguishes required career credits from delivery year. CLE and CLC have no designated grade levels; learners may begin in Grades 10, 11, or 12. The [ordinary CLE registry entry](https://www.bced.gov.bc.ca/datacollections/course_registry_web_search/run-details.php?courseCode=3201860) provides the Grade 10 administrative host used here.

CLE's 4-credit and two-part 2+2-credit reporting options, including French counterparts, share one curriculum. The [verified reporting inventory](career-life-education/README.md#reporting-options) gives public primary registry links and credit/language mappings for CLE, CLEA, CLEB, CLEF, CLEAF, and CLEBF. Adult-only CLE12 and CLEF12 are excluded.
