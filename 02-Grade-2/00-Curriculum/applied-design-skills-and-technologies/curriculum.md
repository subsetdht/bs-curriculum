# Applied Design, Skills, and Technologies - Grade 2

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 2](../README.md) > Applied Design, Skills, and Technologies

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial expectations under the Required Areas of Learning Order [S4], sections 1-2. [S2] supplies immersion-policy context; [S3] is separately labelled CSF implementation guidance.

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`), not a statement about classroom language or the suitability of an English resource for French instruction.

**Learning objective:** Develop foundational design-and-making habits by noticing possibilities, trying ideas, using tools safely, and reflecting on products and collaboration.

**Prerequisites:** Curiosity and play provide entry points. No prior programming, device experience, or specialist design skill is required by this reference.

**Materials:** None are needed for reading. A later making activity must specify suitable materials, tools, accessibility options, and safety arrangements.

**How to use:** [S1] explicitly combines **K-3 ADST Curricular Competencies with grade-level content from other areas of learning**. This record applies that band to Grade 2; it does not define a new Grade 2-only technology syllabus. Map the relevant partner-subject content as well as ADST when using the local IDs, which are repository references rather than ministry codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Curiosity can start a design, and purposeful play provides opportunities to develop the skills needed to make it.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, curiosity in design and developing skills through play.

### BI-02

Tools and technologies can expand what people are able to accomplish.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, technologies and human capabilities.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Explore needs or opportunities, draw on interests and experiences to propose ideas, develop ideas with others, and choose one to pursue.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Applied Design: Ideating.

### CC-02

Choose tools and materials and make a product using familiar procedures or approaches demonstrated by others. Try changes, solve emerging problems, and incorporate useful ideas through trial and error.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Applied Design: Making.

### CC-03

Decide how to share a product and with whom. Demonstrate it, explain the design-and-making process and its contribution to people or the environment, and evaluate its success using personal preferences. Reflect on effectiveness when working individually and in a group.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Applied Design: Sharing, including individual/family/community/environment contributions.

### CC-04

Use materials, tools, and technologies safely in both physical and digital settings. Extend existing skills and acquire new ones through play and collaborative work.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Applied Skills.

### CC-05

Investigate how simple, available tools and technologies can extend one's own capabilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Applied Technologies.

## Content

### C-01

The source supplies an integration instruction rather than a separate ADST content list: use the K-3 design, skill, and technology competencies with **Grade 2 content from another learning area**. That partner subject supplies the knowledge being investigated; ADST adds the design-and-making processes.

**Source locator:** [S1], introductory content instruction identifying ADST K-3 competencies, grade-level content, and cross-curricular learning.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Do not turn this grade-banded curriculum into required Grade 2 coding, robotics, software, or device units. Technologies include tools beyond computers, and the source's elaboration allows a product to be a process, system, service, or designed environment as well as a physical object.

The Grade 2 entry is not a menu of separate technology courses. Its Content section remains here to preserve the integration rule, not to suggest that ADST has no curriculum relationship.

Official elaborations clarify ideating, products, sharing, and technologies. They do not prescribe a product, a purchase, a public sale, or a single way to participate. Any later cross-curricular material should cite both this record and the specific Grade 2 content it uses.

This is a regular program-area reference, not an individual learner checklist. See the [program-order exceptions](../README.md#provincial-requirements-and-delivery) and [S4], section 3.
