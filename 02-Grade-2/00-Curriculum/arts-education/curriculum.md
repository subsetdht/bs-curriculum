# Arts Education - Grade 2

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 2](../README.md) > Arts Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial subject expectations under the Required Areas of Learning Order [S4], sections 1-2. [S2] and [S3] provide immersion-policy and CSF implementation context.

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`), not a prescribed classroom language.

**Learning objective:** Develop a personal creative voice through dance, drama, music, and visual arts, connecting artistic exploration, technical growth, interpretation, and communication.

**Prerequisites:** Play, movement, listening, and making offer starting points. The reference assumes no specialist training or prior performance experience.

**Materials:** None are needed to read the reference. Later arts materials must identify accessible participation options, safe tools, and relevant permissions.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for official expectations and elaborations across all four art forms. They are components of this Grade 2 curriculum, not four elective course choices. Local IDs are stable repository references, not official ministry codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Creative expression helps learners develop an individual identity and voice.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, creative expression, identity, and voice.

### BI-02

Artistic inquiry creates space to try unfamiliar possibilities and learn from outcomes that are not entirely predictable.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, inquiry and risk-taking; associated informed-choice elaboration.

### BI-03

Dance, drama, music, and visual arts offer distinct ways of making meaning, allowing people to connect across places and times.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, art forms as languages and connections with others across places and times.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Explore artistic elements and ways of working with movement, materials, tools, and technologies. Create alone and with others through imagination, inquiry, experimentation, and purposeful play, drawing on personal, community, and cultural experience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Exploring and creating.

### CC-02

Notice and discuss how artists work, develop creative processes and technical skills in different art forms, and reflect on connections between artistic work and other experiences.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Reasoning and reflecting.

### CC-03

Interpret symbols and use creative works to communicate ideas, feelings, stories, and observations. Describe and respond to art, experience and document works in different ways, and share them with increasingly developed engagement with the content.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Communicating and documenting.

## Content

### C-01

Explore how dance organizes the moving body through space, energy, timing, relationships, and form. In drama, connect character, time, place, and plot with the tension that gives an event dramatic meaning.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, elements in dance and drama.

### C-02

Explore musical relationships among pulse, duration, rhythmic patterns, tempo, pitch, timbre, dynamics, form, and texture, attending to how these elements contribute to a musical experience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, elements in music.

### C-03

Use visual features such as line, shape, texture, colour, and three-dimensional form, and investigate how pattern, repetition, rhythm, and contrast organize a design.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, visual-art elements and principles of design.

### C-04

Learn processes and techniques for working with artistic materials, tools, and technologies. Explore notation for sounds, ideas, and movement, different dramatic forms, and symbols that express particular meanings.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, arts processes/materials/technologies, notation, dramatic forms, and symbolism.

### C-05

Encounter traditional and contemporary Indigenous arts and ways of making, as well as local works and traditions from diverse cultures, communities, times, and places.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, traditional/contemporary Aboriginal arts and diverse local works and artistic traditions.

### C-06

Understand individual and shared responsibilities for a safe setting when creating, experiencing, or sharing art, including physical and emotional safety.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, personal and collective responsibility in a safe learning environment, with elaboration.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The source describes its listed artistic elements as non-exhaustive. Read the terms and elaborations in their art-form context: music, dance, and visual arts can use the same word differently. Grade 2 includes features such as dramatic tension, musical timbre and texture, and visual contrast; it is not only a repeat of a drawing or craft focus.

Artistic risk-taking is an informed creative choice with room for unexpected results, not unsafe behaviour. The source does not prescribe a performance, particular instrument, software package, or public sharing of personal experiences.

Traditional and contemporary Indigenous arts remain part of the subject. Studying a cultural work is not permission to copy a design, perform a song, or adapt a story or ceremonial practice. Use appropriate attribution, relationships, and permissions.

This is a regular program-area reference, not an individual learner checklist. See the [program-order exceptions](../README.md#provincial-requirements-and-delivery) and [S4], section 3.
