# Career Education - Grade 2

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 2](../README.md) > Career Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial expectations under the Required Areas of Learning Order [S5], sections 1-2. The program sources [S3] and [S4] provide immersion-policy and CSF implementation context.

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`), separately from classroom instruction.

**Learning objective:** Develop self-knowledge, realistic learning goals, constructive work habits, and understanding of how relationships and different roles contribute to communities.

**Prerequisites:** Everyday experiences of learning and working with others offer starting points. Neither employment experience nor an occupational choice is expected.

**Materials:** None are needed to read the reference. Later materials should not require disclosure of family employment or finances.

**How to use:** [S1] is the Grade 2 view of the **K-3 standards**, confirmed by the corresponding heading and expectations in the official compilation [S2]. Apply the band to Grade 2 without inventing extra age-specific standards. Local IDs are repository references for curriculum mapping, not ministry codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Discovering one's qualities, interests, and abilities can strengthen confidence.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, self-discovery and confidence.

### BI-02

Community life depends on relationships, varied roles and skills, and people working toward shared goals with clear, respectful communication.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, connected communities, effective collaboration, and different roles and skills.

### BI-03

Learning builds capabilities throughout life, including through experiences that may not initially seem connected to a future career.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, learning as skill development and a lifelong enterprise.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Recognize and value personal attributes, skills, interests, and accomplishments. Set realistic learning goals and work toward achieving them.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, self-knowledge and setting/achieving realistic learning goals.

### CC-02

Recognize the importance of positive relationships, share ideas, information, feelings, and knowledge, and collaborate respectfully and constructively toward common goals.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, relationships, sharing, and working with others.

### CC-03

Use work habits and organizational skills appropriate to one's stage of development.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, effective work habits and organizational skills.

### CC-04

Recognize contributions and responsibilities in schools, families, and communities, and the basic skills involved in different local jobs. Connect learning now with possibilities throughout life and in future careers.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, roles/responsibilities, community job skills, and the importance of learning.

## Content

### C-01

Learn ways to set goals and explore how trying unfamiliar things can contribute to self-discovery. The goal-setting elaboration includes identifying steps and sources of support.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Personal Development: goal-setting strategies and risk-taking in self-exploration, with elaborations.

### C-02

Build cultural and social awareness while learning about local jobs and roles and responsibilities at home, school, and in the community. The awareness elaboration includes identity, cultural differences, and honouring Indigenous traditions.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community and its cultural/social-awareness elaboration.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Career Education has its own **Personal Development** and **Connections to Community** content in the K-3 band. Do not apply ADST's separate content-integration instruction to Career Education or replace its content with a job-listing exercise.

The compilation [S2] also includes later grades. Only its K-3 section supports this record; later-band expectations about public digital identity, workplace change, or formal career planning are not imported here.

At this age, constructive risk-taking means trying something unfamiliar within appropriate support and safety, not hazardous behaviour. Official examples remain at [S1]. Learning about community contributions does not require a child to select an occupation or disclose private experiences.

This is a regular program-area reference, not an individual learner checklist. See the [program-order exceptions](../README.md#provincial-requirements-and-delivery) and [S5], section 3.
