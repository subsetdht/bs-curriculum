# Physical and Health Education - Grade 2

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 2](../README.md) > Physical and Health Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared provincial subject expectations under the Required Areas of Learning Order [S4], sections 1-2. The program sources [S2] and [S3] supply immersion-policy and CSF implementation context, not an inference from document language.

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`).

**Learning objective:** Develop movement skills and healthy practices while learning to recognize exertion, support well-being, maintain respectful relationships, and respond to unsafe situations.

**Prerequisites:** Start from each learner's movement, communication, and self-awareness experiences. No fitness threshold, particular body type, or ability is assumed.

**Materials:** None are needed to read the reference. Later activities require their own accessible equipment, adaptations, supervision, and safety planning.

**How to use:** Follow the locators to [S1] for official expectations and elaborations. Local IDs identify curriculum summaries, not medical recommendations, fitness assessments, or ministry codes.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Daily moderate-to-vigorous activity supports well-being, and learning to move in varied activities develops physical literacy. Physical, emotional, and mental health influence one another.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, daily activity, physical literacy, and interconnected dimensions of health.

### BI-02

Healthy routines and safety strategies help protect both oneself and other people.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, healthy personal practices and safety.

### BI-03

Communicating effectively and managing emotions help people build and sustain healthy relationships.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, communication, emotions, and relationships.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Develop fundamental movement skills in varied activities and environments and use ways to monitor exertion. Practise safety, fair play, and leadership, and explain what contributes to positive experiences of activity.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Physical literacy.

### CC-02

Participate daily in moderate-to-vigorous physical activity and identify opportunities at school, home, and in the community. Explore healthy eating and other aspects of healthy living, including how to find health information and support services.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Healthy and active living.

### CC-03

Describe avoidance and assertiveness strategies for unsafe or uncomfortable situations. Demonstrate respect, recognize characteristics of positive relationships, and explain how outdoor activity connects people with community and environment.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Social and community health.

### CC-04

Identify and use strategies that support mental well-being and help with feelings and worries. Describe how personal skills, interests, and preferences contribute to self-identity.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Mental well-being.

## Content

### C-01

Learn techniques for movements in place, travelling movements, and controlling objects. Develop knowledge of participation in individual and paired activities, rhythmic activities, and games.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, non-locomotor/locomotor/manipulative skills and types of physical activity.

### C-02

Learn how to monitor exertion and recognize effects of activity on the body. Connect activity, nutrition, and illness-prevention practices with health and well-being.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, exertion monitoring, activity effects, and health-promoting practices.

### C-03

Learn ways to obtain health information and understand that substances can have different effects, requiring strategies to prevent harm.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, accessing health information and effects of substances/harm prevention.

### C-04

Develop knowledge of protective strategies for hazardous, unsafe, or abusive situations. The source elaborates boundaries, seeking help, and protecting personal information; its relationship competencies also introduce consent and respecting another person's refusal.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, hazardous/unsafe/abusive situations; Social and community health elaborations on avoidance/assertiveness, respectful behaviour, and positive relationships.

### C-05

Learn about managing and expressing emotions and factors that shape self-identity, including how people see themselves and their capabilities.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, emotions and factors influencing self-identity, with elaboration.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Health learning includes safety, respectful relationships, and mental well-being as well as movement and nutrition. Do not reduce it to exercise or require disclosure of personal health information or unsafe experiences.

The core participation statement is daily activity at moderate-to-vigorous intensity. The physical-activity elaboration under health-promoting practices gives **60-90 minutes each day**; this source guidance is not an individualized exercise prescription or a new assessment target created by the repository.

The source's safety elaborations include an initial understanding of consent, personal boundaries, trusted sources of help, and recognition of abusive or grooming behaviour. Consult the official guidance for its detail; this record supplies no role-play, personal questioning, or treatment instructions.

Activities named in elaborations are examples, not a required sport list. Accessible participation and individual circumstances must be addressed in any later activity design.

This is a regular program-area reference, not an individual learner checklist. See the [program-order exceptions](../README.md#provincial-requirements-and-delivery) and [S4], section 3.
