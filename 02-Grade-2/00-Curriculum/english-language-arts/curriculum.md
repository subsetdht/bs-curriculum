# English Language Arts - Grade 2

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 2](../README.md) > English Language Arts

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language. The Required Areas of Learning Order [S4], section 2(a), distinguishes French Language Arts for immersion and Francophone students. Use the [program overview](../README.md#choose-a-program) to select the appropriate Grade 2 record.

**Document language:** English (`en-CA`).

**Learning objective:** Build understanding and purposeful expression across reading, listening, viewing, writing, speaking, and representing, connecting texts with identity, family, and community.

**Prerequisites:** Shared reading, conversation, and emerging knowledge of print and sounds can support learning; this reference does not establish admission criteria or a commercial reading-level threshold.

**Materials:** None are needed to read the reference. Any later text selection must be reviewed separately for suitability, accessibility, and rights.

**How to use:** Consult [S1] for the complete expectations and elaborations. Use local IDs for traceable curriculum references, not as official standard codes or a required sequence of texts.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Language invites playful, creative exploration. Curiosity about words, stories, and the world can bring enjoyment and new understanding.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, creativity and joy, language play, and curiosity and wonder.

### BI-02

Stories and other texts connect personal experiences with family and community, while making room for each person's individual story.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, connections through texts and everyone's unique story.

### BI-03

Listening and speaking allow people to share experiences and develop connections with one another.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, listening and speaking.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Read fluently at Grade 2 level and use information, prior knowledge, and developmentally appropriate reading, listening, and viewing strategies to construct meaning.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Comprehend and connect: grade-level fluency, information sources, prior knowledge, and strategies.

### CC-02

Connect personal and cultural experience with texts through active reading, listening, and viewing. Notice how text structures serve different purposes, how stories are built, and how stories contribute to personal, family, and community identity.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Comprehend and connect: text structures, active engagement, identity, personal connections, and story structure/elements.

### CC-03

Recognize the role of First Peoples stories in relationships with family and community.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Comprehend and connect: story in First Peoples cultures.

### CC-04

Exchange ideas and perspectives to develop shared understanding. Plan and create communications in varied forms, making choices for particular audiences and purposes.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Create and communicate: exchanging ideas and planning/creating communication forms.

### CC-05

Create stories and other texts that deepen understanding of self, family, and community. Communicate in sentences using most Canadian spelling, grammar, and punctuation conventions, and explore the process of preparing and sharing oral stories.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, Create and communicate: creating texts, sentences and conventions, and oral storytelling.

## Content

### C-01

Learn how story elements, literary devices, and presentation features contribute to a text. Develop vocabulary for discussing texts, including the way written and visual information is arranged.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Story/text: story elements, literary elements/devices, text features, and associated vocabulary.

### C-02

Develop approaches to understanding reading and communicating orally, alongside ways to reflect on learning. Use writing processes that support developing and improving a message, including attention to revision, editing, and audience.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Strategies and processes: reading, oral language, metacognition, and writing processes.

### C-03

Explore how voice and gesture shape oral messages and how word patterns and word families support language learning.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions: oral-language features and word patterns/families.

### C-04

Develop legible letter formation and spacing, sentence structure, and written conventions. The source's sentence elaboration includes compound sentences; its conventions elaboration addresses capitalization and sentence-ending punctuation.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Language features, structures, and conventions: letter formation, sentence structure, conventions, and their elaborations.

## Elaborations and boundaries

The curriculum uses "text" broadly, including oral, written, visual, digital, and combined forms. Story is not restricted to print or to fiction. The source elaborates fluency through comprehension, phrasing, and attention to punctuation; this summary does not assign a publisher's level, reading speed, or uniform timetable.

The strategy elaborations describe reading, conversation, reflection, and writing in greater detail. They remain at [S1]; this is a core summary, not a reproduction of every strategy or an endorsement of a particular literacy program.

Use the separate [French Immersion](../french-immersion-language-arts/curriculum.md) and [Francophone](../francophone-language-arts/curriculum.md) records for those Grade 2 pathways. The provincial immersion policy [S2] places K-2 instruction in French; the CSF's own elementary guidance [S3] introduces English Language Arts in Grade 4. That CSF schedule is provider guidance, not a new province-wide requirement.

Oral storytelling and engagement with First Peoples texts do not grant permission to retell, adapt, or reproduce protected works. No required story collection or personal disclosure is specified here.

This is a regular program-area reference, not an individual learner checklist. See the [program-order exceptions](../README.md#provincial-requirements-and-delivery) and [S4], section 3.
