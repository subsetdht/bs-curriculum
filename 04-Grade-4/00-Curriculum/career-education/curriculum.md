# Career Education - Grade 4

[Curriculum index](../../../curriculum-index.md) > [Grade 4](../README.md) > Career Education

[Big Ideas](#big-ideas) | [Curricular Competencies](#curricular-competencies) | [Content](#content) | [Sources and rights](#sources-and-rights)

## Scope and use

**Programs:** English-language, Early French Immersion, and Francophone; shared subject requirements under [S3].

**Document language:** English; the reviewed curriculum source is in English. See the [program overview](../README.md#choose-a-program) for classroom-language distinctions.

**Learning objective:** Connect developing self-knowledge, public identity, relationships, and work habits with thoughtful choices and achievable goals.

**Prerequisites:** Prior reflection on interests and experiences may help. No occupation choice, employment experience, or public digital profile is required by this reference.

**Materials:** None are needed to read this reference.

**How to use:** Read [S1] for official expectations and elaborations. The ministry compilation [S2] groups these standards as **Grades 4-5**; this record applies that band to Grade 4, not the K-3 band or a separate Grade 4-only syllabus. Local IDs support traceable references, not ministry coding or a prescribed teaching order.

## Big Ideas

### BI-01

Personal choices shape how others see a person, while understanding one's strengths helps make goals more purposeful.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, public identity and exploration of strengths and abilities.

### BI-02

Leadership and decision-making are relational: listening respectfully and drawing on family or community support can improve how people address problems.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, respectful leadership and relationships as sources of support and guidance.

### BI-03

Habits developed in everyday learning can contribute to success both now and in future work.

**Source locator:** [S1], Big Ideas, learning/work habits and short- and long-term personal and career success.

## Curricular Competencies

### CC-01

Recognize personal qualities, interests, achievements, and changes in ability over time. Identify people who can support further growth, and consider how peers, family, and community influence choices.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, personal attributes and growth, learning support, and social influences on choices and goals.

### CC-02

Examine how personal identity intersects with the identity presented publicly, including digitally. Consider possible benefits and harms of that public presence while respecting differences among classmates.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, personal/public digital identities and respect for differences; digital-identity elaboration.

### CC-03

Use inventive thinking to address problems. Set realistic goals over shorter and longer periods, identify a route toward them, monitor progress, and connect effective work habits with results.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, innovative problem solving, goal-setting and monitoring, and work habits.

### CC-04

Demonstrate safe behaviour in different environments and investigate how technologies are changing work, using questions directed to oneself and others.

**Source locator:** [S1], Curricular Competencies, safe behaviours and technology in the changing workplace.

## Content

### C-01

Develop practical knowledge of goal-setting, problem solving, and decision-making, including the communication and initiative involved in emerging leadership.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Personal Development: goal-setting, problem-solving/decision-making strategies, and emergent leadership.

### C-02

Consider identity and cultural differences alongside the responsibilities of different generations. Cultural and social awareness includes honouring Indigenous traditions, without treating all communities' practices as interchangeable.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community: cultural/social awareness, its elaboration, and generational roles/responsibilities.

### C-03

Recognize hazards and the purposes of safety rules across home, school, and community settings.

**Source locator:** [S1], Content, Connections to Community: safety hazards and rules.

## Elaborations and boundaries

Career learning here is preparation for thoughtful participation and ongoing growth, not a demand to settle on an occupation. The source's goal-setting examples illustrate approaches rather than prescribing a single planning framework.

The public-identity topic includes a digital footprint but also ways a person represents self and community. Studying it does not require publishing a learner's information, opening a social-media account, or discussing private family circumstances.

Use the Grades 4-5 portion of [S2], not the compilation's later workplace expectations. Official elaborations remain at [S1]. Cultural examples need appropriate local guidance and permissions.
