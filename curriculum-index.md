An available summary does not mean every course or program is complete; consult the landing's coverage status. Use [program navigation](#browse-by-program) for the established summary pathways.

The catalog's course-list and core-curriculum routes are kept distinct. `6T` and `7T` have separate transition landings under Grades 6 and 7. [CLE, CLC, and Additional Offerings](curriculum-special-entries.md) have named landing sections rather than guessed grade assignments. Individual courses beneath course-list landings still need to be inventoried and summarized.

## Filling the landings

The next content work is to fill the existing landings, not rebuild the navigation. Start from a page marked **Needs summary** or **Needs course inventory**, review its official source and program scope, then follow the [landing-page workflow](curriculum-format.md#filling-a-landing-page). Keep the landing URL stable and add the completed record beside it or within an appropriate named course folder.

## Referencing a specific item

Use a descriptive, clickable reference such as [Mathematics 1: C-01](01-Grade-1/00-Curriculum/mathematics/curriculum.md#c-01), not a bare `C-01` outside its own record. Its canonical repository reference is `bc-g01-mathematics#c-01`.

Subject records have quick links to Big Ideas, Curricular Competencies, and Content, plus a return route to the grade and this index. The [Grade 1 subject table](01-Grade-1/00-Curriculum/README.md#subjects-and-learning-standards) also links directly to each section.

The IDs belong to this repository, not the ministry. See the [linking convention](curriculum-format.md#linking-to-records-and-items) and [record template](templates/curriculum-record.md) before adding new material.
